REV B DESIGN DECISIONS AND REVIEW FINDINGS
19 November 2026

This record distinguishes completed design corrections from unresolved
prototype validation. Manufacturer references are in the corresponding review
notes and parts-complete.csv.

CORRECTED IN THE COMPONENT DEFINITION

1. Meter SPI and isolated-supply pin assignments were checked against the
   ADE7953 and ADuM6421A datasheets. Meter return remains neutral-live and
   must not be connected to isolated GND.
2. The CT burden is permanently attached across the CT secondary. Disconnecting
   the PCB therefore does not intentionally leave the CT unburdened.
3. Meter input calculations distinguish the CT's 60 A maximum input from the
   circuit's approximately 26 Arms ideal unclipped measurement range.
4. Added the permanent 33-ohm PSU load for specified light-load operation.
   Revised the complete load allocation to 5.7 W and local ambient to <=50 C.
5. Corrected the coil-circuit connector from a 3.81 mm type with inadequate
   III/2 voltage rating to Phoenix 1836189, 5.08 mm, 320 V under III/2.
6. Separated watchdog heartbeat from explicit ARM latch clock. A restored
   interlock or ordinary heartbeat cannot intentionally re-arm the latch.
7. Buffered the high-high supply sense before the MCP23017. Changed its lower
   divider resistor R87 to 100k for input margin at U35; R86 remains 22k.
   Discharge with a floating source is approximately 1 ms; use >=5 ms selector
   OFF dwell and independent software mode-change cancellation.
8. Corrected BAT54WS-7-F to its two-pin SOD-323 package; do not substitute the
   differently pinned three-pin BAT54W part.
9. Corrected ADS1115IDGSR to the 10-pin DGS / TSSOP-10 3x3 mm land pattern.
10. Replaced direct grounding of spare MCP port pins with 100k pull-downs.
11. Replaced TLV803EA30DBZR with STM1001TWX6F. Worst-case original release
    threshold could exceed the buck's 3.1845 V lower rail. ST's specified
    3.00-3.15 V threshold provides 34.5 mV DC release margin. Pin mapping is
    changed: 1 RESET, 2 VCC, 3 GND. Falling threshold touches the MCU minimum;
    supply transients and reset propagation still require bench testing.
12. AC-blocking capacitors are included in all seven field probe conductors
    to avoid sustained electrode DC from a stalled excitation output.

13. Added J34, a small 3.3 V I2C / two-GPIO expansion header with 25 mA
    reserved supply current. USB recovery and upgrade/configuration design
    requirements are recorded in programmability.txt.
14. Replaced all discrete indicators with a purchased 0.91-inch OLED module,
    Waveshare 14657, 36 x 12.5 mm. J33 is now four-pin GH, with reverse numeric
    mapping to the module PH header. The completed previous LED board is
    archived, not part of the current design. Reserve 100 mA for the display
    pending measurement; total allocation is now 5.7 W. Short scheduled display
    transfers must preserve the probe ADC deadlines.

15. Replaced the unverified SS14-13-F ordering entry with manufacturer-verified
    Diodes B140-13-F. Footprint, diode polarity and nets are unchanged. The
    stated 0.5 V maximum forward drop applies at 1 A and 25 C; temperature
    and the complete coil path remain prototype validation items.

REMAINING REVIEW / TEST ITEMS

1. Actual contactor coil rating and inductive inrush/holding load; suppression
   and allowable contact stress; starter-specific integration and enclosure fit.
2. Coordinated mains fault protection, insulation system, surge/EMC, altitude,
   pollution environment, component clearances and safe enclosure/mounting.
3. Main-board routing completion and native DRC status. A placement pass does
   not establish a routed, manufacturable board. See hardware/checks.
4. Derived package footprints and connector mating parts need supplier review.
5. R2 heat spreading, closed-enclosure PSU ambient and all hot restart cases.
   B140 plus field wiring reduces K1 coil voltage; catalog pickup is at 23 C.
6. STOP/MAN/AUTO divider logic has limited transient/contact-drop margin.
   Require debounce, no arming on ambiguity, and complete supply-corner tests.
7. No direct overload/contactor auxiliary feedback. Use manual-reset overload
   and STOP/OFF before overload reset; do not promise hardware latching of
   all external coil-supply interruptions.
8. Probe cable capacitance, leakage, actual water conductivity, fouling,
   long-cable surge and worst-case multi-electrode loading need tests.
9. Meter calibration and phase orientation; startup clipping detection;
   pump-specific dry-run envelope and level-progress limits.
10. Firmware implementation, timing, persistence, watchdog service policy,
    no-unexpected-start behavior, Matter commissioning and LAN dashboard.
11. Actual Apple Home / Aqara interoperability and RF performance through
    the closed enclosure with the intended external antenna.

No physical pump, mains, thermal, insulation or RF test has been performed.
