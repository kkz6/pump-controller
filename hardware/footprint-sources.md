# Footprint sources

The KiCad board projects include their own symbol and land-pattern tables. The standard land patterns derive from the KiCad 10 library. The controller also uses project-specific drawings for the Omron P6B-04P socket, power converter, ESP32 module, isolation ICs, display connector and panel STOP switch. The manufacturer references are provided in each board's component BOM. Custom model files accompany the individual projects. Install the standard KiCad 10 3D model library to display components that use its STEP files.

The upstream licensing exception and attribution apply to the redistributed KiCad library data. [License](KICAD-LIBRARY-LICENSE.md) · [KiCad footprints](https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-footprints)

`PumpRevE:R_2512_6332Metric_DNP_NoPaste` retains the standard 2512 copper, mask, courtyard and body geometry for the deliberately unpopulated R2 preload, with both paste layers removed. This prevents the supplied stencil from depositing solder on its two unused lands. R2 remains DNP in the schematic, PCB and BOM.

`Inductor_SMD:L_Sunlord_SWPA4030S` follows Sunlord SWPA1102230000 Rev.11, Fig.4-1/Table4-1: 1.90 mm gap, 1.10 mm pad width and 3.70 mm pad height (pad centres at +/-1.50 mm). It replaces the mismatched Würth land for L1. [Sunlord-authored drawing](https://www.alfatec.de/fileadmin/productfiles/datasheets/sunlord_swpa4030s_series.pdf) · [Assembly correction review](../verification/pcba-review-2026-11-24/R4/README.md)

R3 relay revision (2026-11-24): `PumpRevE:G5RL_1_E_HR` follows the G5RL manufacturer eight-terminal drawing: coil 1/8, NC 2/7 at x=15 mm, COM 3/6 at x=20 mm, NO 4/5 at x=25 mm, rows 7.5 mm apart, eight 1.3 mm holes. Both terminals of every contact are routed. [Omron source](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g5rl.pdf). The body WRL is a 29 x 12.7 x 15.7 mm envelope. `PumpRevE:MKDS3_3_508_Power` derives from the KiCad Phoenix MKDS-3-3-5.08 land, with 2.9 mm pads and the specified 1.3 mm holes. Pin order is 1 NO, 2 COM, 3 NC; both blocks rotate 180 degrees so their wire entries face out over the top board edge. Only the front silkscreen outline is clipped to the board edge; pads, fabrication outline, courtyard and body model retain the manufacturer's dimensions. [Phoenix 1711738](https://www.phoenixcontact.com/en-in/products/printed-circuit-board-terminal-mkds-3-3-508-1711738).

R4 compact placement (2026-11-24): K1/K2 rotate to 180 degrees and J30/J35 to 0 degrees, facing out of the bottom edge. The terminal pin-1 silkscreen triangle moves inward by 1 mm to clear that edge; copper, mask, drill, courtyard, body dimensions and physical pin assignments are retained. Tank and inlet headers rotate to 180 degrees at the top edge.
