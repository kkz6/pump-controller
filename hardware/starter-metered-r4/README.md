# Onboard AC voltage control for a single-phase DOL starter

The socketed main PCB and five-button OLED PCB serve one 1 HP starter. This cabinet revision uses the main board's ADE7953 for programmable high and low voltage. The original STOP and overload interrupt the factory contactor coil directly. The reference assumes a conventional three-wire starter with a **24 V DC coil**, suitable mechanically linked auxiliary contacts and OEM approval to use one common control supply. The actual starter's coil and factory diagram are unknown. This is an engineering prototype for that specific interface. [Editable KiCad project](starter-metered-r4.kicad_pro) · [Six-page schematic](cabinet-schematic.pdf) · [Required parts and cost](cost.md)

## Power and measurement

The metering point is the protected feeder **after the two-pole motor isolator and before the motor contactors**. On the field sheet, F3 protects the auxiliary line to PS2 and the main J1 line terminal. Their neutral comes from the same feeder. J1 is the existing controller power connector. It provides voltage before START and during a run. It measures neither the motor-terminal drop downstream of the contactors nor the 24 V DC rail. The motor and its capacitors carry current through the starter. One total motor-feed conductor passes through the CT.

PS2 is the sole isolated 24 V DC source. F2 supplies the existing factory STOP/overload/coil path and the cabinet permission circuit. XP1 is a distribution point on that rail. An existing separately energized factory control supply is incompatible with this drawing. Never parallel it with PS2. A sustained common supply loss drops the coil and the permission memory. Opening the service isolator also removes J1 and PS2 input power. Bench programming uses the main board's documented isolated J41 service input and J40 USB data port. [Socketed main](../main-socketed/README.md) · [USB service connections](../main-integrated/README.rst)

The main board uses its existing input fuse, four series 249 kΩ resistors, 1 kΩ divider leg, filter, ADE7953 U10 and isolated SPI connection U11. Both sockets remain on the board. This revision changes cabinet wiring and the control software. The checked main and OLED Gerbers can be reused. ## Voltage limits and recovery

For controller-operated MANUAL and AUTO, initialize through `metered_dol_configure`. Commission the low/high thresholds, hysteresis, recovery interval, and the pair of true-RMS reference millivolts and simultaneous raw counts from the installed input. Use settings inside the motor manufacturer's permitted operating range and the supplies' ratings. This profile limits the configurable cutoffs to the common supply's 85–264 V AC rated range. Verify linearity at both thresholds with a traceable instrument. Each effective threshold must lie inside the qualified measurement range. [PS2 ratings](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/HDR-60/HDR-60-SPEC.PDF)

After meter reset is complete, initialize `metered_spi_init` on the dedicated SPI2 bus. U3 uses GPIO6 for clock, GPIO7 for MOSI, GPIO14 for MISO and GPIO18 for chip select. Wait for meter-ready or allow at least 100 ms from known stable meter power before setup. The driver sets the required timing register, verifies it, and reads the unsigned 24-bit VRMS register at 0x21C. The assembled application must use `metered_gpio_step` on the control cycle, apply its returned drive immediately even on a read error, and keep the display independent of the stop path. `mains_mv` and `voltage_status` are available to the OLED. The repository contains the control component and a disabled target build fixture. A complete fitted pump application, settings storage, display integration and bench calibration remain open.

The first valid reading at or beyond either cutoff withdraws run permission. A failed SPI transaction, invalid timing readback, stale reading or saturated value also inhibits running. Recovery requires successive fresh readings inside the hysteresis band for the configured interval. A healthy reading alone cannot issue START. The operator must give a fresh start authorization. The controller operates the original interface only in HOST. The **physical LOCAL transfer** gives control to the factory buttons and keeps independent STOP/overload operation. Programmable voltage cutoffs apply in HOST, including panel MANUAL. LOCAL provides no firmware voltage cutoff.

## Factory stop and remaining limits

The original normally closed STOP and overload contacts are in series upstream of every normal pickup/holding branch. Their linked auxiliaries separately trip cabinet permission. A quick STOP inside the reference timing envelope clears the hardware enable, including with either PCB command contact welded or the software frozen. Releasing STOP or resetting the overload cannot give a fresh START. The holdoff, relay proofs, reset, dual contactors and service interlock are retained from the previous cabinet. [Physical contact table](contacts.csv) · [Underlying holdoff and relay timing assumptions](../starter-budget-r4/README.rst)

The circuit was modeled for settled common supply outages. During a brief dip the power supply output may remain energized. A separate break confined to the factory coil wiring can let the coil drop while the cabinet remains enabled. With pickup held, restoring that wire can restart the motor. This is a separate event from pressing the original STOP. An in-range reading stuck inside the meter, a frozen processor or a welded command does not guarantee an automatic voltage stop. The original STOP/overload path remains necessary. No protection rating or successful physical test is claimed.

A qualified starter technician must verify the coil label, factory circuit, permitted common supply, fuse coordination, wire sizes, inrush and margin for all coils on PS2, auxiliary contact travel, installed flyback, power supply hold-up, and relay withdrawal clearance. With the motor disconnected and a representative load, test brief STOP and overload opening/reset with pickup held, then supply loss and return, service isolation, and high/low/failing meter readings. The model's shortest credited effective switch break is 16 ms with the documented auxiliary timing. These timing limits need measurement on this particular starter.
