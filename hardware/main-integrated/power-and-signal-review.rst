R4 supply and guard input margins
=================================

The receiver adds a 25 mA allowance to the main isolated 5 V rail. The cabinet supplies the permission relay KFB1, the panel monitoring relay KPS1 and the 24 V outputs. The 5 V AC/DC converter supplies both command relay coils. The motor and starter contactors draw their power outside the PCB.

Simultaneous steady-state allocation
------------------------------------

* At 3.3 V, reserve 500 mA for the ESP32-C6 and flash, 100 mA for OLED, 15 mA for the isolator digital side, 5 mA for the probe electronics, 2 mA for the I/O expander and 18 mA for supervisors, gates, input transitions and pullups. This totals 640 mA. The earlier 665 mA estimate included 25 mA for an expansion header absent on this PCB. The OLED allowance requires measurement on the actual all-pixels-on module. Espressif lists a 382 mA Wi-Fi transmit peak at 3.3 V and 25 °C. The commissioned Zigbee configuration also needs a load-step check. See the `original allocation <../../design/power-budget-review.txt>`_, `revised logic allowance <../../design/rev-e-electrical-review.rst>`_ and `Espressif module data, table 6-4 <https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-c6-mini-1_mini-1u_datasheet_en.pdf>`_.
* Add 10 mA on 3.3 V for both status LEDs, position inputs, USB logic and heartbeat sensing. Ignoring forward drops, both 1 kΩ LED resistors together draw at most 6.94 mA at 3.432 V. Both shorted position returns draw at most 0.64 mA, R244 at most 0.35 mA and heartbeat sensing under 0.17 mA. U41's specified static consumption is below 1 µA. The 650 mA allocation uses 2.789 W from 5 V at 3.432 V and an assumed 80% buck efficiency. See the `TI data switch <https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ts3usb30e.pdf>`_.
* On 5 V, carry 250 mA for the metering isolator's converter, 130 mA for both G5Q coils together and 5 mA for the original control dividers. Another 15 mA covers panel/key wetting, revised bias and U40 load-switch quiescent current. The earlier panel increment was 0.06 W including both key returns, equivalent to 12.7 mA at 4.75 V. See the `panel allocation <../../design/panel-rev-d-integration.rst>`_ and `TI load switch <https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22919.pdf>`_.
* For the receiver, bound both 560 Ω optocoupler LED resistors by 18.94 mA total at 5.25 V, 1% low resistance and zero forward drop. Both 4.7 kΩ pullups add at most 2.26 mA. The 115 kΩ/10 kΩ and 33 kΩ/10 kΩ reference dividers add 0.17 mA together. U301 adds 0.8 mA and panel sensing adds 0.26 mA. These bounds sum to less than 22.5 mA. Round up to 25 mA, or 0.132 W at the upper rail. This counts LED-on and pullup-low loads simultaneously. The combined direct 5 V allocation is 425 mA. See the `TI LM393B electrical characteristics <https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm393b.pdf>`_ and `Vishay VO617A <https://www.vishay.com/docs/83430/vo617a.pdf>`_.

Add the permanent minimum load R2, using its low tolerance value of 32.67 Ω. For a rail V, total current is 2.7885/V + 0.425 + V/32.67 amperes. Total power is voltage times that current. Maximum current is at the low endpoint and power increases with voltage over this interval.

The calculated smallest margins are 42 mA to the 1.2 A limit and 0.136 W to the 6 W limit, at different rail corners. At 115–277 VAC, the 5 V model's derating curve gives 6 W up to 50 °C local ambient and 5 W at 65 °C. The latter is insufficient. Record U1 local air, R2 case and PCB temperature, rail minima, inrush, RF bursts, display startup, buck efficiency and coil pickup/dropout in the closed starter under the most severe intended line and temperature corners, with both coils energized. Additional field loads at J52/J32/J41 have no allocation. The cabinet 24 V supply has its own coil-inrush and thermal budget. See `RECOM RACM06E-05SK/277 selection and derating curves <https://recom-power.com/pdf/Powerline_AC-DC/RACM06E-K_277.pdf>`_.

Input margin and loss of controller power
-----------------------------------------

D301 and D302 each use one BAV199W junction, anode 1 toward the signal, cathode 3 toward a 10 kΩ/10 kΩ input divider. Pin 2 stays open. An upstream 1 kΩ resistor is in series with each divider. Both heartbeat and raw panel STOP use this load. Each comparator drives its optocoupler through a MOSFET. U302/U303 pins 1/2 are the LED and pins 4/3 the cabinet collector/emitter. The cabinet cannot sustain the receiver LEDs through a normal DC path. KFB1 uses a cabinet-powered coil at pins 1/16 and dry COM/NO at 4/8. Verify this with an ohmmeter and a current-limited 24 V fixture. See `Nexperia diode pinout <https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/BAV199W.pdf>`_, `Vishay optocoupler connections <https://www.vishay.com/docs/83430/vo617a.pdf>`_ and the `Omron relay bottom view, page 4 <https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g5v_2.pdf>`_.

At the driving net before R196 or R197 the design threshold is Vf + Vref × (1 + (1 kΩ + 10 kΩ)/10 kΩ). With 1% resistors and a 4.75–5.25 V logic supply, the heartbeat reference spans 0.373–0.428 V and the panel reference 1.088–1.240 V. Allow 5 mV comparator error at IN+ for offset and bias. Bracketing diode drop between zero and 0.90 V gives the room-temperature engineering bounds below. Nexperia specifies 0.90 V maximum at 1 mA and 25 °C. Calculated input currents here are below 0.26 mA. The high estimate assumes forward drop does not increase at lower current. Temperature extremes and interference require measurement.

* Below 0.76 V the heartbeat input should read low. Above 1.82 V it should read high.
* Below 2.25 V the panel permission should read low. Above 3.55 V it should read high.
* U301 has no positive feedback. Noise close to a threshold can create extra edges. Capture transitions with the actual harness and 24 V coils switching. Confirm the watchdog rejects stuck-high, stuck-low and out-of-window rates, and that raw panel STOP drops KPS1. Measure hot/cold timing including relay flyback and coupled interference. An in-band spurious heartbeat can satisfy the frequency monitor. Both direct cabinet trips must still open motor permission.

The diodes oppose current from comparator inputs toward an unpowered MCU or opened STOP return. A still-driven signal toward an unpowered comparator goes in the forward direction. A zero-voltage clamp fault at its input could draw up to 0.316 mA from a 3.432 V heartbeat or 0.483 mA from a 5.25 V panel return through the minimum 10.89 kΩ series path. Leakage, stored charge, intermediate supply voltages and a powered service fixture require physical checks. Run isolated tests with 24 V present while logic 5 V is absent or ramping, with 3.3 V decaying separately, with individual panel wires open and with a connected USB host. Measure +5V, +3V3, HB_24, PANEL_24 and KFB1/WD1/KPS1 release. Require HOST to lose permission on controller loss and a fresh manual restart sequence after recovery. In LOCAL and HOST, prove the original cabinet STOP and independent high-high float drop both motor contactors with the command contacts bridged on a low-voltage simulator. See `TI comparator limits <https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm393b.pdf>`_.
