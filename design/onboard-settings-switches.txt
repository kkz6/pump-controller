Rev C onboard settings switches
==============================

SW3 SET/RESET and SW4 NEXT/+ use B3U-1000P, a two-terminal,
top-actuated, normally open SMT tactile switch without locating boss.
The selected standard KiCad footprint is
Button_Switch_SMD:SW_SPST_B3U-1000P, cached with its original library license.

Manufacturer evidence:
https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-b3u.pdf
https://components.omron.com/system/files/2023-01/datasheet_pdf/A162-E1.pdf

The published minimum applicable load is a reference value of 10 µA at 1 V;
it is not an unconditional contact-reliability guarantee. Nominal closed
contact current is 3.3 V / (10 kΩ + 1 kΩ) = 300 µA for SW3 and 5 V / (22 kΩ + 33 kΩ) = 90.9 µA
for SW4. Validate low-current switching under the actual enclosure
humidity, contamination and service conditions. Maximum published bounce
is 5 ms; software debounce must exceed it with margin.

Body is 3 × 2.5 mm; total actuator height is 1.6 mm, nominal actuation force 1.5 N.
Standard cached pads are 0.9 × 1.7 mm on 3.4 mm center spacing; compare the
manufacturer land drawing during fabrication/assembly review. No pin
numbers are marked on the physical switch; the SPST contact is symmetric.

SW3: top side, center (85, 12) mm; pad 1 RESET_N, pad 2 GND.
SW4: top side, center (85, 18) mm; pad 1 AUTO_RETURN, pad 2 STOP_RETURN.
Coordinates use the PCB upper-left datum; both footprints are at 0 degrees.
The settings buttons are in the isolated right-edge strip. Their actual
insulated access path, clearance from cables/connector plugs, and support
against actuation force require enclosure/mechanical verification.

J32 external wiring is STOP 1–2, MODE 2–3 and START 5–7 only. Pins 4 and 6
remain internally connected for the onboard functions but are reserved:
do not connect external wires to them. SW4 is inactive while physical STOP
is held; release STOP after its hardware ARM-clear event before navigation.
Mode/settings cannot arm the pump; a separate fresh START remains required.
