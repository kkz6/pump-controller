Pump controller Rev C — power allocation and interface review
Reviewed 2026-11-19

POWER ALLOCATION
This is a conservative component allocation for simultaneous operation, not a
measured consumption result or a guaranteed all-fault maximum. It reserves
25 mA at 3.3 V for the planned J34 internal expansion header only. J2 is a
service/reference interface and J33 serves the budgeted OLED; neither
provides an additional external-load allowance. Verify J34 fit on the prototype.

3.3 V loads                              Reserved current
ESP32-C6, RF transmission and flash             500 mA
J33 OLED display, engineering reservation       100 mA
ADuM6421A safe-side digital circuits             15 mA
Probe analogue front end, mux and ADC             5 mA
MCP23017                                         2 mA
Supervisors, logic, pull-ups and margin             8 mA
J34 internal expansion                          25 mA
TOTAL                                          655 mA

The ESP32 module specifies a 382 mA Wi-Fi TX peak at 3.3 V/25 C; 500 mA is
an engineering reservation with margin. The buck is rated 1 A. Reserve 80%
conversion efficiency until measured, giving 3.3*0.655/0.80 = 2.702 W from 5 V.
The 25 mA expansion allowance alone adds 0.103 W nominal (approximately
0.104 W), or 0.107 W at the buck's +4% power-save upper voltage tolerance.

Direct 5 V loads                        Reserved current
ADuM6421A integrated isolated converter         250 mA
Both G5Q-1A4 DC5 relay coils                    130 mA
Physical control dividers and margin              5 mA
TOTAL                                          385 mA

The 250 mA converter allocation uses ADI's full-output-load maximum for 5 V
input/3.3 V output, though ADE7953 plus isolated logic need approximately
16 mA maximum at 3.3 V under the configured low-speed SPI conditions.
G5Q nominal coil current is 40 mA each at 23 C, resistance tolerance +/-10%;
130 mA for both includes a conservative allowance for cold copper and supply
tolerance. No contactor coil or motor current is supplied by this 5 V rail.

Total at nominal rails: 2.701875 + 5*0.385 = 4.626875 W, about 925 mA at 5 V.
Using +4% on 3.3 V and +5% on 5 V gives 2.80995 + 2.02125 = 4.8312 W before
the minimum-load resistor. The +4% buck bound supersedes the earlier +2%
estimate. The 80% buck efficiency and reserved MCU current are assumptions
to verify with startup, maximum RF duty, the OLED, both relays and J34 load.

The Waveshare 0.91inch OLED Module SKU 14657 replaces the discrete indicator
panel. No guaranteed maximum module current was found in its official product,
wiki or schematic documentation. Its 100 mA allowance is an engineering
reservation, not a manufacturer limit or measured value. Measure all-pixels-on,
maximum permitted contrast, charge-pump startup, cold/hot behavior and voltage
corners before qualification. Do not infer module current from the SSD1306
controller logic-current specification. See oled-selection.txt.

PERMANENT MINIMUM LOAD — FINAL SELECTED SOLUTION
R2 = Bourns CRM2512-FX-33R0ELF, 33 ohm 1%, 2 W, 2512, from +5V to GND.
At 4.75 V and +1% resistance: 4.75^2 / 33.33 = 0.677 W.
At 5.25 V and -1% resistance: 5.25^2 / 32.67 = 0.844 W.
Thus R2 alone maintains more than the 0.6 W minimum load for specified PSU
regulation, throughout that voltage/tolerance envelope. Normal rail dynamics
must still be verified at startup and during load steps.

REVISED TOTAL WITH OLED AND J34: 4.8312 W + 0.844 W = 5.6752 W.
Round to 5.7 W (approximately 1.14 A at nominal 5 V). This is below the 6 W /
1.2 A PSU rating with only 0.3 W allowance remaining. This supersedes previous
5.3/5.4 W complete-board allocations and the pre-bleeder 4.4 W figure. Do not
allocate more external loads from the remaining allowance without re-review.

RACM06E-05SK/277 rating, 115-277 VAC input:
Local ambient            Available power   Margin against 5.7 W
Up to 50 C                    6.0 W                 0.3 W
65 C                          5.0 W                -0.7 W

REQUIRE <=50 C measured ambient immediately around the module in this design.
The 65 C thermal envelope considered before R2 is no longer acceptable for the
conservative full-load allocation. The actual normal consumption will be lower,
but it does not replace the design allocation without measurements.

R2 THERMAL LAYOUT REQUIREMENT
Bourns rates this part at 2 W at 70 C with 300 mm2 combined solder-pad and trace
copper. Its rating falls linearly to zero at 155 C. Reserve that heat-spreading
copper, respect both rail nets, and verify case/PCB temperature at 0.844 W in
the assembled enclosure. Place R2 away from probe front end, precision metering
resistors/reference, RF module/antenna and electrolytic capacitors. Do not place
it against a plastic cover or wire insulation. The resistor rating does not
replace thermal verification of the surrounding PCB.

REPLACEMENT SCREEN
The similarly sized RAC05-05SK lists 0% minimum load but is OVC II. Its /277
variant retains OVC III yet carries the same below-10% output-specification
disclaimer. Retaining RACM06E with R2 preserves the selected OVC III module and
footprint. Revisit a zero-minimum-load module only as a separately qualified
power-stage revision, rather than silently downgrading the input environment.

PINOUT AND WIRING REVIEW
- U1: pins 1/2 = line/neutral; pins 5/4 = +5 V/isolated ground. U2 fixed-3.3 V
  FB ties to AGND; VOS senses the output; exposed pad is ground.
- ADuM6421A power and SPI mapping matches Table 25. VDDP is 5 V; VDD1 is 3.3 V;
  VSEL ties to GNDISO for 3.3 V output. The isolated converter positive and
  return pass through separate beads into M_3V3/M_GND; M_GND is neutral-live.
- GPIO17/module pad 30 is ARM_CMD. J2 pin 4 is ARM_TEST, not UART receive.
  GPIO16 is UART transmit logging; USB pins 12/13 provide programming/debug.
- Updated HH sensing passes through SN74LVC1G17 before the MCP23017. This
  retains a defined Schmitt threshold; the Rev C coil feed has no selector diode.
- J30 has been changed to MC 1,5/2-G-5,08, Phoenix 1836189, rated 320 V under
  III/2. This resolves the original 3.81 mm header's insufficient 160 V III/2
  rating for a 230 V starter. Apply the same rating to its mating plug.
- Supply J1 from the protected incoming line and neutral, before the contactor
  power contacts. J30 belongs in the existing contactor-coil control path,
  preserving the overload and stop protection; no hold-in branch may bypass it.
- J32: +5 V -> NC STOP -> STOP_RETURN -> J31 high-high loop. MODE is a
  momentary NO sensing contact from STOP_RETURN to pin3. START is NO
  between pins5/7. Pins4/6 are reserved; onboard SW4 NEXT/+ uses the former
  AUTO input and SW3 SET/RESET uses the former RESET input. Preserve old
  power/capacitor contacts and use suitable isolated auxiliary contacts on
  existing button actuators. SET/RESET is not the ESP32 hardware reset.
  D33/D34 are removed; the 130mA relay reservation already covers both coils
  at the full supply, so the 5.7W allocation does not increase.
- J31 is the independent NC high-high float circuit. J10/CT wiring is mains
  referenced and enclosed/insulated; the burden remains permanently attached
  across the CT when unplugged. J20/J21 probes and J33 OLED harness use
  only the isolated low-voltage domain. Never bond M_GND to GND.
- J2 is internal service access, with 3V3 as sense/reference. Use native USB
  only under the documented isolated bench-power service arrangement.

FUSE FINDINGS — NO COMPONENT CHANGE MADE
The present Bel 0ADKP1000-RE is a T1A 600 VAC axial fuse. Its interrupting
capacity is 200 A at 600 VAC; it is not a general high-fault-current branch fuse.
The installation's prospective fault current and upstream protection must be
coordinated. A series 22-ohm resistor is not proof of adequate fuse breaking
capacity, especially for faults ahead of that resistor.

A smaller candidate is Schurter UMT-H 3403.0274.11: 1 A time-lag, 16 x 5.3 mm
SMD, 277 VAC, 1500 A breaking rating at 277 VAC under its UL conditions.
It is NOT a documented drop-in equivalent for RECOM's agency-tested T1A
420/600 VAC arrangements. Use only after confirming the intended system
approval/test path with the PSU supplier.

A size-preserving alternative is Eaton BK-S505H-V-1-R, 1 A time-delay,
5 x 20 mm axial, 600 VAC: 1500 A breaking at 250 VAC but 100 A at 600 VAC.
Do not assume its 1500 A rating applies at 264/277 VAC. It improves the
published 250 VAC interrupting figure, not every installation condition.
For higher prospective faults, coordinate a suitable upstream high-breaking
branch device; choosing a tiny PCB fuse alone does not establish system SCCR.

FINAL FUSE FOOTPRINT CHECK — RETAIN PRESENT F1
Littelfuse 477 does not provide the requested 1500 A interruption at 500 VAC
for the 1 A part: its 1500 A figure is specified at 400 VDC, while the relevant
500 VAC figure is 100 A. Schurter SHT does specify 1500 A at 500 VAC but uses
a 6.3 x 32 mm body, not the present 5 x 20 mm / 27.94 mm axial footprint.
Neither is a verified no-layout-change upgrade. No F1 change was made.
The 22-ohm R1 limits only faults downstream of R1. It cannot limit a short
between F1 and R1. Upstream interrupting rating alone also does not establish
coordination with this fuse: check the complete protective arrangement.

SUPERVISOR TOLERANCE CORRECTION — SAME PACKAGE, PIN REMAP REQUIRED
The original TLV803EA30DBZR can release as high as
3.08 x 1.02 x 1.015 = 3.1887 V, exceeding the TPS62162 power-save lower
static limit 3.3 x 0.965 = 3.1845 V. This permits a corner where permission
never releases. TLV803EA29DBZR is pin compatible but its 2.93 V threshold
can fall to 2.8714 V, below the ESP32 module's 3.0 V operating minimum.

Selected replacement: ST STM1001TWX6F, open-drain active-low, SOT-23-3,
3.08 V nominal, 3.00–3.15 V threshold over -40 to +85 degrees C, release
delay 140–280 ms. Datasheet describes assertion/release about this threshold
without an additional specified hysteresis increment. Static release margin
is therefore 34.5 mV against the buck's stated power-save lower limit.
This is NOT a pin-compatible substitution: use 1 RESET -> SAFE_CLEAR_N,
2 VCC -> +3V3, 3 VSS -> GND, with the same SOT-23 footprint and local rerouting.

MAX803TEUR+T is not a documented alternative: Analog Devices MAX803 is
open-drain in SC70 only; its SOT-23 family members MAX809/810 are push-pull.
The similarly named onsemi NCP803SN308T1G must not be confused with it.

The ST minimum threshold exactly meets the module's 3.0 V floor, and reset
propagation is finite. The DC comparison is not a guarantee that all rail
dips are caught before the MCU leaves its recommended range. Retain firmware
brownout reset, test load steps/slow ramps/fast collapses, verify boot-time
inhibition and require a fresh START after a trip. The supervisor must not
be described as a certified safety function. ST lists this exact MPN active;
availability must be reconfirmed at procurement. The current core.json now
contains STM1001TWX6F with the stated pin remap; verify the generated PCB
and assembled part orientation against it before applying power.
