PROBE FRONT END — REV B ENGINEERING PROTOTYPE
19 November 2026

Status: component-value prototype design, not a field-qualified protection product.
Authoritative connectivity is probe-front-end.json. This sheet covers only the
isolated low-voltage probe circuit, not mains insulation or motor switching.

1. CONNECTIONS AND MEASUREMENT LIMITS

J20: pin 1 tank common; pins 2..5 L1..L4, ordered bottom to top.
J21: pin 1 inlet sensing electrode; pin 2 a separate nearby inlet reference.
The existing single inlet wire cannot provide a conductive measurement without
a verified return electrode. Neither neutral, motor body nor protective earth is
the designed return. Each of the seven field conductors has a series DC-blocking
capacitor. Field ESD devices reference only isolated controller GND.

Five tank bands are available, not a continuously measured volume. An inlet wet
indication means liquid is present, not that it is moving. Bare probes cannot
distinguish dry water from an open wire; the independent high-high inhibit and
motor underload protection remain necessary.

2. EXCITATION AND REFERENCE, EXACT VALUES

U20 = TLV9004IPWR, TSSOP-14. Pins 4/11 = isolated +3V3/GND.
R100/R101 = 10k/10k, 0.1%, create 1.65V nominal at PROBE_BIAS_DIV;
C100 = 100nF to GND. U20A: +in pin3 at divider, -in pin2 joined
to out pin1. R102 = 47 ohm from pin1 to PROBE_BIAS; C101 = 1uF
to GND. Feedback is before R102 to isolate capacitive loading.

R103 = 27k from PROBE_EXC to PROBE_TX_SET; R104 = 43k from that
node to PROBE_BIAS. R105 = 100k PROBE_EXC pulldown. With a 0/3.3V
GPIO, TX_SET switches between 0.6364V and 2.6636V, nominal.
U20B is a unity buffer at pins 5(+),6(-),7(out), for tank excitation.
U20D is a second unity buffer at pins 12(+),13(-),14(out), for inlet.
Both +inputs connect TX_SET; each -input joins its own output.

Each driver path is:
  op-amp OUT -> 2.2k -> DRIVE node -> 2uF DC blocker -> CLAMP node
  -> 4.99k -> 4.99k -> common electrode terminal.
BAT54S at DRIVE: pin1 GND, pin2 +3V3, pin3 DRIVE.
PESD5V0S1BA from CLAMP to GND, bidirectional.
The 4.99k parts are CRCW12064K99FKEA, 1206, 1%, 0.25W.
Each common blocker is two 1uF capacitors in parallel: C106 || C108
for tank, C107 || C109 for inlet. This improves mixed wet-load margin.

3. FIVE IDENTICAL SENSE CHANNELS

For each L1..L4 and INLET:
  terminal -> 10k -> 10k -> CLAMP node -> 1uF DC blocker -> SENSE.
  SENSE -> 47k -> PROBE_BIAS.
  SENSE -> 22k -> ADC node (the respective mux input).
  ADC node -> 1nF C0G -> PROBE_BIAS.
  PESD5V0S1BA from CLAMP to GND, bidirectional.
  BAS70-04 at ADC: pin1 GND, pin2 +3V3, pin3 ADC.

Terminal series parts: CRCW120610K0FKEA, 1206, 1%, 0.25W.
Other resistors: 1% 0603 except the 0.1% reference divider.
All DC-block capacitor parts: TDK C2012X7R1H105K125AB, 1uF, 50V, X7R,
10%, 0805. Five sense paths each use 1uF; two common paths each use
2 x 1uF = 2uF. The model includes a -30% effective-capacitance case.
Do not substitute a substantially lower capacitance without repeating analysis.
All sense channels stay connected to their individual loads; the mux selects
the measurement, not the electrode excitation. This avoids switching charge
into a newly selected, previously floating field electrode.

4. MULTIPLEXER, ADC AND FIRMWARE TIMING

U21 = TMUX1208PWR, TSSOP-16. Pin13 +3V3; pin14 GND; pin2 EN tied
to +3V3; pin3 NC. Pin1=A0, pin16=A1, pin15=A2; each address has
a 100k pulldown. S1..S5 select L1,L2,L3,L4,INLET respectively.
S6 = bias reference, S7 = tank driver, S8 = inlet driver. Exact
pin numbers are in the JSON and TI datasheet. Selected signal D pin8
goes to U20C +in pin10. Its -in pin9 joins out pin8.
R114 = 1M from mux output to PROBE_BIAS gives a defined idle bias.
This introduces up to approximately 7% additional selected-channel loading;
the conservative RC model below loads EVERY channel this way simultaneously.

U22 = ADS1115IDGSR, VSSOP-10. Pin8 +3V3, pin3 GND, pin1 ADDR=GND
(0x48). Pins9/10 = I2C SDA/SCL. Shared 3.3V I2C pullups are on the main
controller, not duplicated in this sheet. The bus is also used by MCP23017
(0x20) and the selected Waveshare 0.91-inch 128x32 I2C OLED at J33. Confirm
the exact carrier revision/address before assembly; it must not conflict
with either device. Pin2 RDY has 10k pullup; the current controller uses OS polling
unless an explicit GPIO connection is added in a later design revision.

AIN0(pin4) from U20C out through 100 ohm, 10nF shunt at ADC.
AIN1(pin5) from PROBE_BIAS through 100 ohm, 10nF shunt.
AIN2(pin6) from tank excitation OUT through 100 ohm, 10nF shunt.
AIN3(pin7) from PROBE_BIAS through 100 ohm, 10nF shunt.
Each IC has its own 100nF bypass. Local bulk = 4.7uF.

Normal acquisition: AIN0-AIN1, PGA +/-2.048V, 860 samples/second,
single-shot conversion. Excitation: 25Hz, equal 20ms positive/negative
half-cycles. After each edge, wait until 7ms. Select L1 at 7ms, L2 at
9ms, L3 at 11ms, L4 at 13ms, inlet at 15ms. Allow at least 100us
analog settling after mux selection; then start and complete a fresh ADC
conversion. At 860SPS the datasheet oscillator tolerance means about 1.3ms
must be allowed, plus I2C overhead. Use conversion-ready, not a guessed
fixed read delay. The 2ms slots require 400kHz I2C and bounded firmware
latency. Discard any sample that straddles an excitation edge or misses
its slot. Stop/re-arm logic must regard persistently missing sensing as a
fault; an old ADC register is not a fresh measurement.

Shared-bus scheduling with the OLED:
- Use one deadline-aware I2C owner. ADC conversion control/readout and local
  protection-input reads take priority over display traffic. Do not let a
  display driver hold the bus lock across an entire screen update, wait
  indefinitely for a peripheral, or disable the excitation/control timer.
- A 128x32 monochrome frame is 512 data bytes. At 400kHz, data and ACK bits
  alone take 11.52ms; framing/commands/software commonly extend this toward
  13ms. Such a blocking frame cannot fit the 7ms pre-scan interval.
- Limit each display data transaction to 16 payload bytes. Address, control
  byte and 16 data bytes take 162 clocks, or 0.405ms, before additional
  commands/software. Establish a measured worst-case transaction bound;
  use 0.5ms as the initial scheduling budget only if that bound is verified.
- Initial schedule: reserve 0..1ms after each excitation edge for control
  servicing; permit OLED chunks only in 1..6ms; leave 6..7ms quiet and
  7..19ms for probe/diagnostic acquisition. No OLED transfer may overlap a
  conversion or delay the next excitation edge. Launch a chunk only when
  its complete command/data budget fits before the boundary. Otherwise
  defer it. Four 0.5ms chunks per half-cycle can distribute a full frame
  over about 160ms; one or two updates per second is sufficient for the
  normal human-readable status screen. Up to five updates per second is
  conditional on the measured complete-frame/control budget; never exceed it.
- Render into RAM outside critical acquisition work and send only changed
  regions where practical. Initialization/recovery also uses bounded
  transactions while permission is off. Do not silently lower the bus to
  100kHz: that invalidates both display and 2ms ADC slot budgets.

Freshness and fault handling:
Tag each accepted conversion with excitation edge/polarity, channel, start
time and completion time. Check write/read acknowledgements and OS-ready
for the newly started conversion; never reuse a previous ADC register after
a missed slot, bus error or timeout. Reject an entire affected bipolar pair
and do not fill a five-cycle average by repeating its previous value. Keep
an explicit expiry for each raw pair and averaged state in the protection
state machine; maximum age must be validated against the required stop
time, not inferred from display refresh. Inhibit a new start on missing,
expired or ambiguous sensor data. Confirmed shared-bus failure or expired
protection data must clear RUN_CMD/PERMIT_D and latch a fault, rather than
waiting for the watchdog's potentially 2.24s timeout. Service the watchdog
only after successful required sensing/control checks.

An absent OLED that NACKs can be disabled while valid ADC and local-input
acquisition continue. An OLED holding SDA/SCL low compromises the shared
protection bus and therefore requires fail-off. Perform bounded bus recovery
with permission off, reinitialize the affected devices, discard incomplete
averages and require fresh sensing plus user re-arm. Display failure must
not suppress hardware STOP/OFF/high-high action or convert invalid sensing
into a displayed/stored valid level.

Pullups and physical bus limits:
The main board has 3.3k pullups on SDA and SCL. The selected Waveshare
carrier has 4.7k pullups to its internal 3.3V regulator rail. Their nominal
parallel resistance is 1.939k, drawing about 1.70mA at a zero-volt LOW,
or 1.50mA at VOL=0.4V if both rails are 3.3V. Using 3.432V for both rails,
1% low main resistors and 5% low carrier resistors gives about 1.82mA at
zero volts, below the ADS1115's 3mA test current for VOL<=0.4V. The carrier
regulator's actual output may differ from the main rail when powered from
3.3V; measure both HIGH and LOW levels. This calculation does not verify
other modules, level shifters, leakage, power sequencing or cable capacitance.
Inspect/measure the assembled carrier and total pullup resistance before
installation. Remove or leave unpopulated redundant carrier pullups if the
combined load is excessive; retain an effective pullup that meets both sink
and rise-time requirements. Never permit pullups to an external 5V rail.

For an ideal RC bus, 30..70% rise time is about 0.8473*R*C. The 300ns
400kHz limit corresponds to about 107pF with 3.3k alone or 183pF with 1.939k,
before tolerances. Measure rise time and LOW level at the farthest device;
these capacitance calculations are not approved cable-length ratings. Use
a short internal display cable, routed away from probe analog traces and
the contactor/coil wiring, with a close ground return. Check the cable's
actual pin order against J33. The selected carrier's order is 1=SCL,
2=SDA, 3=GND, 4=VCC; wire each net explicitly because J33 and generic OLED
modules can use a different order.

The OLED charge pump/refresh can inject supply and ground noise even with
no I2C update in progress. Compare ADC bipolar amplitudes and classification
with the display absent, blank, all pixels lit, and rapidly changing data,
including contactor switching and worst-case radio traffic. Scheduling alone
does not establish analog compatibility. Probe-calculation.txt is unchanged:
its RC model does not simulate OLED noise or shared-bus firmware timing.

For each channel A = (positive reading - negative reading) / 2. Average
over five complete 40ms cycles = 200ms. Ideal 50Hz interference cancels
between paired half-cycles; a five-cycle average also rejects ideal 60Hz
pickup. Frequency drift, clipping, nonlinearity and EMI are not removed by
this mathematical argument; actual contactor/noise tests are mandatory.
Initial classification: wet A >= 0.100V; dry A <= 0.060V; intermediate
values are ambiguous, not automatically wet or dry. Normalize thresholds
for actual supply/excitation amplitude or include the supply tolerance.
Validate sensor confidence before allowing a new pump start. Debounce and
ambiguous-state handling must be coordinated with the root control logic.

Use the spare 17ms slot for one diagnostic conversion if timing permits;
alternate S6 zero/reference, S7 and S8 excitation checks. Both polarities
must arrive and the amplitude must be plausible. At boot or after a waveform
interruption, discard the first 1s of sensing. Coil permission stays off
until sensing and the other protection inputs are valid. A frozen GPIO
leaves only a finite capacitor transient, then essentially no electrode
current except component leakage. The system watchdog must still stop the
motor because a DC-blocked but frozen circuit does not provide valid sensing.

5. CALCULATIONS, PROTOTYPE ENVELOPE AND WEAKNESSES

probe_calculation.py is an explicit backward-Euler linear RC model with a
25us step and 1s settling. Its results are in probe-calculation.txt.
It includes shared-common loading by all four tank channels, cable capacitance,
coupling capacitance and a conservative 1M load on every mux input.
It does NOT model electrode polarization, contamination, external ground
potentials, temperature-dependent diode leakage, ADC digital filter weighting,
op-amp saturation/stability, EMI or surge protection. The timed ADC window is
an approximation, not an ADS1115 silicon model.

Electrical commissioning target: wet electrode resistance <=100k at the
chosen spacing/water; dry insulation >=1M. Nominal modeled cable: 3nF
per conductor to GND, 3nF common-to-sense, 0.5nF sense-to-sense. A severe
stress case uses 10nF, 10nF and 2nF respectively. These are model values,
not a cable manufacturer's guaranteed per-metre characteristics.
Initial cable-length TARGET <=30m, 0..60C enclosure ambient. Neither is
a released rating: confirm measured cable capacitance and field behavior
before fitting to an installation. Longer lines or very pure water can
require different excitation/current thresholds or a remote sensor interface.

Shorted-probe normal currents are small: a single ideal conducting branch has
12.18k common + 20k field series + 47k load, before capacitor impedance.
Excitation step/79.18k = 25.6uA; charging conditions can temporarily raise
this, but passive impedances still limit it. Four parallel branches load
the common driver; the simulation explicitly includes that interaction.
Series blockers prevent a firmware-stalled output from sustaining DC. Real
capacitor and suppressor leakage means the true steady current is not exactly
zero; damaged parts or external wiring faults are outside that guarantee.

The TMUX recommended signal range is 0..VDD; absolute maximum is -0.5 to
VDD+0.5V. BAS70-04 specifies <=410mV at 1mA, 25C. The 22k resistor limits
secondary clamp current after the primary field clamp. This supports the
chosen protection approach but is NOT a qualified worst-case transient design:
verify temperature corners, rail uplift and powered/unpowered injection.
The ADS1115 absolute analog range is -0.3 to VDD+0.3V regardless of PGA
range; it sees only buffered in-rail analog outputs, never the field node.

The low-leakage ESD diode is not a direct-lightning protector. Standard 1206
resistors have limited voltage and pulse energy; the pair is not permission
to connect a probe cable to mains. Final EFT/surge requirements may require
higher pulse-rated resistors, additional arresters and a defined chassis path.
Clamp currents can enter the 3.3V rail; validate rail absorption with the
actual regulator and controller, including power off. No surge test has yet
been run. Sensor wiring and mains wiring must remain physically separated.

7. REQUIRED BENCH CHECKS BEFORE MOTOR INSTALLATION

Use an isolated low-voltage bench supply first. Verify ADC references and
excitation amplitudes. Substitute known 0, 10k, 100k, 200k, 1M and open
probe loads, with all wet-count combinations and the maximum cable emulator.
Test disconnected common, crossed probes and each single input short.
Verify AC current decays after excitation GPIO is held high or low and
after MCU reset. Test 50/60Hz pickup with safe isolated injection; record
diode leakage and wet/dry separation hot and cold. Test actual water and
probe fouling. Only then evaluate contactor switching, power dips, ESD/EFT
and appropriate surge tests with suitable equipment and qualified handling.
