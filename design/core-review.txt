CORE CIRCUIT CHECK — REV C
19 November 2026

Scope: component pin assignments, metering values and the revised independent
ARM latch/STOP/high-high circuit. This is a design review and calculation,
not a hardware test, insulation certification or commissioning record.

FUNCTIONAL WIRING

The on-board RACM06E-05SK/277 supplies isolated 5V. It passes through the
normally closed STOP contact to STOP_RETURN, then directly to J31.1 and the
normally closed high-high float before K1_COIL_PLUS. D33/D34 are removed.
STOP or high-high opening physically removes K1 coil supply and clears ARM.
A missing high-high float is not replaced by software; a fitted link provides
continuity only, not independent overflow protection.

Front controls retain the START/STOP actuators and add one MODE button.
J32 STOP NC is pins1/2; START NO pins5/7; MODE NO pins2/3. Pins4/6 remain
reserved and must not be wired to external buttons. New SW3 SET/RESET is
on the PCB between RESET_N/GND. SW4 NEXT/+ is on the PCB between
AUTO_RETURN/STOP_RETURN. Both are Omron B3U-1000P SPST-NO tactiles.
MODE is sense-only and toggles MANUAL/AUTO; it never arms or starts.
Press and release physical STOP before mode/settings changes. New START
is required afterward. Settings keys are internal service controls, usable
only with suitable isolated service power or a verified insulated access.
Preserve original capacitor-start/power contacts; use suitable separate
isolated auxiliary contacts on the existing button actuators where possible.
All re-used panel switch contacts must be disconnected from their old mains
wiring before connection to J32. No contact may mix the new low-voltage
controls and the old mains control circuit.

Identify and preserve the starter's motor overload protection and its verified
trip path in the contactor-coil circuit. Board J30 inserts the K1 and K2 normally open contacts
in series in that coil circuit. The original mains START/seal-in bypass must
be removed or rewired so it cannot energize the coil around either relay.
This revision's manual mode uses local electronics; it does not depend on
Matter or the network, and it does not bypass controller protection.

ARM_CMD clocks D=1 into U31 only following a fresh user re-arm. It is separate
from watchdog HEARTBEAT. K1 gate = ARM_Q AND software PERMIT_D. Watchdog,
power supervisor, MCU reset or loss of INTERLOCK_OK clears ARM_Q. A return
of power/interlock or the next ordinary heartbeat cannot re-arm it.
After an interlock transition, firmware must first set RUN_CMD/PERMIT_D low,
observe valid mode and protection inputs, and accept a new user action.
Keep ARM_CMD low at boot and never send it as part of periodic servicing.

With R87=100k and C86=10nF, the floating K1_COIL_PLUS sense node has
about a 1ms nominal discharge time constant; verify short physical STOP taps.
The MODE button has no hardware latch-clear path. Software must not claim that
PERMIT_D clears U31: it only inhibits K1. Require a confirmed physical STOP
clear before accepting a mode/menu change, then release STOP to restore the
mode-button supply. Held START at menu exit is not a new start request.

The photographed starter contains two capacitors and a two-pole contactor.
Existing automatic starting is confirmed, but the source of start-capacitor
engagement/disconnection is not identified. J30 controls only the coil loop;
it does not supply a timed capacitor-start contact. Verify that function and
actual motor overload protection before removing the previous controller.

PIN/LOGIC CHECKS

ADE7953: pin26=MISO, pin27=MOSI, pin25=SCLK, pin28=CS. Pins7/8 go high,
pin14 low, and exposed pad joins the live analog/digital ground. Regulator
and reference bypassing, reset 10k/1uF and unused B-channel grounding are
consistent with the manufacturer's connection guidance.

ADuM6421A pin mapping, converter ground separation and VSEL=GNDISO for 3.3V
output are consistent with its datasheet. Maintain the required isolation
barrier and the manufacturer's local converter/ferrite layout; electrical
net separation alone does not establish reinforced insulation.

STWD100NY: WDO1, GND2, EN3, WDI4, VCC5; EN low enables it. Its selected
timeout is 1.6s nominal, 2.24s maximum. SN74LVC1G74 DCU pin assignments
and the new SN74LVC1G08 gate are consistent with the proposed function.
The watchdog must be serviced only after successful fresh control/sensor
checks, not by an independent timer that continues through a stalled loop.

D30/D35 must use the implemented BAT54WS-7-F two-pin SOD-323 part with
pin1 cathode, pin2 anode. Nexperia BAT54W,115 is a DIFFERENT three-pin
SOT-323 part and is not a drop-in replacement.

Rev C removes D33/D34 and their selector diode-OR function. STOP_RETURN
now feeds the high-high loop directly. This removes the former diode drop
but does not waive contact, wiring, transient or hot-relay pickup tests.

The active Rev C design replaces the indicator panel with a 0.91-inch
128x32 I2C OLED. U33 GPA0..GPA7 are intentionally unconnected; configure
them as inputs with their internal pullups enabled. They are not display
drive outputs. J33 is 1=3V3, 2=GND, 3=SDA, 4=SCL. The selected OLED
carrier is 1=SCL, 2=SDA, 3=GND, 4=VCC: the harness maps 1->4, 2->3,
3->2, 4->1 by signal, with different connector families at its ends.
These assignments were checked in the merged circuit; confirm the actual
carrier revision and cable orientation before powering it.

The OLED shares the 400kHz I2C bus with the ADC and local controls. Use
bounded 16-byte display chunks in the acquisition idle interval, normally
1-2Hz updates. Up to 5Hz is conditional on the measured bus/control budget.
Whole-frame blocking writes are excluded. Target at most 0.5ms per complete
display transaction only after measurement verifies that bound.
The selected module's 4.7k pullups in parallel with the main 3.3k give
about 1.939k and 1.70mA nominal LOW current. Measure actual bus levels and
rise time, including the carrier's internal regulator. Probe-front-end.txt
defines scheduling, stale-data fail-off and OLED-noise validation details.
A display NACK alone may disable its updates; a stuck shared bus requires
fail-off because ADC and local input freshness are then compromised.

LOCAL INPUT AND SUPPLY MARGINS

MCP23017 GPIO VIH is 0.8*VDD. STOP/MODE/NEXT use 22k/33k 1% dividers
on the STOP/button supply. Taking the PSU's 2% accuracy, 0.3% line and
1% load terms conservatively together gives 4.835V at 25C. Worst divider
ratio is 0.595190; with 1uA input leakage the minimum high is 2.864V.
TPS62162 power-save +4% permits VDD=3.432V, so VIH=2.7456V. The resulting
119mV input margin allows only 0.200V upstream switch/wire drop.
At 60C, the PSU's 0.05%/K temperature term lowers the calculation to
4.7475V: input margin is about 67mV, or 0.112V upstream drop. These figures
exclude ripple, load transients and PCB drops. A 4% PSU transient can cross
the input threshold. Debounce input state, fail off on unresolved/invalid
mode data, and never arm on an ambiguous mode. This is not a complete
combined-corner qualification. Keep panel control wiring short.

HH uses U35's 5.5V-tolerant Schmitt input, with its rail-to-rail output feeding
MCP GPB5. Final circuit: R87=100k; R86=22k and C86=10nF.
At the illustrative 60C supply corner of 4.7475V, resistor corners and 5uA
U35 input leakage give approximately 3.787V HH high before wiring drop.
The former selector diode is absent. The full-load PSU still requires <=50C.
The maximum corresponding input is about 4.412V, below its 5.5V rating.
The merged circuit values were checked after the change; no hardware
measurement has been made.

Final U32 is STM1001TWX6F: threshold 3.00-3.15V over -40..85C,
open-drain reset, 140-280ms release delay. Its SOT-23 pins are 1=RESET_N,
2=VCC, 3=GND; these differ from the previous TLV803E, and the merged circuit
now uses the ST mapping. The 3.15V maximum threshold is below TPS62162's
3.1845V static minimum by 34.5mV. This resolves the identified static
release overlap; ripple/transients and local supply drops still require
measurement. No extra hysteresis allowance is specified for this ST part.
The 3.00V minimum falling threshold equals the MCU module's minimum supply;
finite reset propagation/glitch rejection means this is not a guarantee
that clear always precedes a sub-3.00V excursion. Test fast/slow brownouts
and supply oscillation while the pump is commanded on; keep the MCU's own
brownout protection enabled and require fresh user re-arm after any reset.

CALCULATED METER VALUES

Voltage network: 4*249k + 1k = 997k total; gain = 1/997.
  230Vrms -> 0.23069Vrms -> 0.32625V peak at VP.
  264Vrms -> 0.26479Vrms -> 0.37448V peak at VP.
  This remains below the voltage channel's +/-0.5V peak range at gain1.
  At 264Vrms, divider current = 0.26479mA.
  Each 249k dissipates about 17.46mW and drops about 65.93Vrms.
  The selected 200V-working resistor rating has normal-operation headroom;
  surge and single-fault behavior still need separate evaluation.

Measurement point: the voltage divider samples L_PROTECTED, after the
controller's 22-ohm series input resistor. The motor power feed does not pass
through that resistor. Controller supply current therefore creates a small,
load-dependent voltage drop/distortion between the sensed node and the motor
supply. Calibrate against the actual starter supply with representative RF,
OLED and relay activity, and quantify the resulting real-power error. Do not
claim precision motor-terminal voltage/power or a fixed accuracy from the
nominal resistor ratio alone. Moving the sensing point would require a new
surge/input-protection review, not just changing a label.

Current network, ideal 1000:1 CT, 6.8 ohm permanent burden:
  6Arms  -> 40.8mVrms  -> 57.70mV peak; burden 0.245mW.
  20Arms -> 136.0mVrms -> 192.33mV peak; burden 2.72mW.
  60Arms -> 408.0mVrms -> 577.00mV peak; burden 24.48mW.
The ADE current channel in this single-ended configuration is limited to
+/-250mV at gain1. Its ideal unclipped limit is therefore about 26Arms,
not 60Arms. The 60A figure belongs to the CT manufacturer's maximum input,
not to this meter's calibrated range. Saturated inrush samples must be
identified/blanked and must never be treated as reliable underload evidence.
Use PGA gain1 for the stated current range; higher gain reduces the range.

The permanent burden stays soldered at the CT when its board cable is
unplugged. The CT surrounds one verified total motor-supply conductor before the
capacitor/winding branches, not an arbitrary COM/RUN/START winding lead. Its secondary,
burden and connector become mains-referenced when connected to this meter,
so keep the entire assembly insulated and inside the appropriate live zone.
CT ratio, phase error and orientation require calibration using an adequate
reference instrument; nominal calculations are not an accuracy guarantee.

The 1k/33nF anti-alias filters have fc about 4.82kHz. Voltage low-leg source
resistance is about 999ohm, closely matching the current channels' filters.
The 27pF crystal capacitors give 13.5pF series capacitance; adding the assumed
4.5pF stray gives 18pF. Confirm oscillator startup and actual frequency on
the assembled PCB rather than treating stray capacitance as known.

REMAINING MATERIAL DECISIONS / VERIFICATION

1. A contactor auxiliary-feedback input is not present in the reviewed core.
   MCP GPB6/GPB7 are spare inputs with separate 100k pulldowns R92/R93,
   not hard-grounded. Add an isolated dry auxiliary-contact input if
   contactor-state verification is claimed. Otherwise RUN must be described
   as observed motor current or a command indication, with the distinction
   explicit. Current sensing cannot prove the contactor's mechanical state.
2. Verify the actual contactor coil voltage, inrush/holding VA and suppression
   against G5Q contact ratings; resistive current ratings are insufficient.
3. Without the former diode drop, K1 has approximately 4.7725V available
   in the 50C static supply calculation before contacts, wiring and MOSFET.
   The approximate copper model gives 4.155V pickup at 50C versus the
   catalog <=3.75V at 23C, leaving about 0.618V before those losses.
   This estimate is not a guaranteed hot-pickup curve. Validate the full
   STOP/float cable loop, ripple and coil self-heating in the closed starter.
   Check flyback-delayed release and physical STOP with stalled software.
4. Test boot, reset, watchdog timeout, MODE requests, short STOP taps,
   high-high trip/recovery, remote pause and resumed MCU execution. None
   may restart automatically without the required fresh re-arm/action.
5. The overload NC is in the external mains coil loop and has no direct trip
   feedback to this board. Opening it does not directly clear ARM_Q. Load/
   voltage software can detect and latch a fault after its detection delay,
   but cannot guarantee prevention of every short external-interruption
   restart. Use a manual-reset overload; press STOP before resetting it.
   Do not describe all external coil-loop faults as hardware-latched.
6. The 33ohm 5V preload dissipates about 0.758W continuously. Thermal
   verification must include this load, the AC/DC module, relays, meter
   isolation converter and RF activity in the actual closed starter.
