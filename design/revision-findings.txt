REV C CHANGES AND REVIEW FINDINGS
19 November 2026

Rev B is preserved at commit dc09030 and archive/rev-b-selector. Its selector
and diode-OR descriptions are superseded by the following focused revision.

1. Replace the maintained selector with a single momentary MODE input.
   Remove D33/D34 and merge MODE_COIL_5V into STOP_RETURN. J31.1 now takes
   the STOP return directly. Two onboard settings tactiles replace the two diodes; 208 main parts.
2. Keep physical NC STOP, independent NC high-high, watchdog, supervisor,
   ARM latch and separate K1/K2 contactor-coil permission paths.
3. Use onboard SW3 SET/RESET and SW4 NEXT/+ with existing MCP inputs.
   J32.4/6 are reserved, left unwired externally; no additional GPIO or IC.
4. Mode buttons cannot clear the hardware latch. Press AND release STOP
   before mode/settings changes; neither button nor menu exit can arm.
   Require a separate fresh START after all buttons have been released.
5. Front panel: existing START/STOP actuators, one new MODE button and OLED.
   Settings keys are on the PCB: SET selects/saves, NEXT advances/increases
   (hold to decrease). Hold SET for entry/back/exit. Internal service access
   must be isolated; capacitor-start contacts on original buttons stay intact.
6. The starter photographs show capacitors and a two-pole contactor. The
   previous system starts automatically, but its start-capacitor switching
   provider, coil voltage, overload path and existing wire identities remain
   to be traced. Do not assume J30 alone replaces every RMG output.
7. New wiring guide includes actual connector pad positions, separate power
   and coil circuits, OLED reverse-numbered harness, and photo limitations.
8. 3D models preserve PCB positions; custom body envelopes and the photographed
   enclosure concept are not mechanical fit acceptance. A lid loom may exceed
   the provisional100mm OLED cable assumption and needs electrical testing.

Unchanged electrical reviews remain in core-review.txt, power-budget-review.txt
and probe-front-end.txt. Firmware, calibration, insulation/protection, thermal,
EMC and assembled-starter testing remain required. Native check results for
Rev C are recorded under hardware/checks; Rev B counts do not apply to Rev C.
