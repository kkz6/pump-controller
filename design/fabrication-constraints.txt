PCB FABRICATION CONSTRAINTS - PROTOTYPE, NOT A RELEASE
19 November 2026

The 90 x 70 mm main board uses four copper layers and components on both
sides. Confirm the final stackup, material, assembly process and all values
below with the selected fabricator before generating a manufacturing order.
No fabricator has accepted this design and no board has been manufactured.

LOW-VOLTAGE GEOMETRY

- General minimum copper track width and spacing: 0.15 mm.
- Selected local QFN escapes may use 0.40 mm via diameter with 0.20 mm hole,
  giving 0.10 mm nominal radial annulus. These are local routing provisions,
  not a reason to shrink the larger vias elsewhere.
- Any signal via overlapping an SMT solder pad must be filled and plated
  over (a qualified via-in-pad process), or rerouted outside the pad before
  fabrication. Tenting alone is not an equivalent treatment. This applies
  to the proposed compact U10 pin18 crystal escape; confirm the final
  via-overlap inventory in the layout report.
- Thermal-via footprints also require 0.20 mm holes. Check the final via
  inventory, finished-hole convention, registration tolerances, via treatment
  and solder-wicking behavior with the assembler.
- Current JLCPCB rigid-board capabilities list 0.20 mm as a preferred minimum
  via hole; a 0.20/0.40 mm hole/diameter combination is within the published
  limits but below the 0.45 mm outer diameter associated with its lower-cost
  option. This is evidence of available manufacturing capability, not approval
  of this board or a commitment to that supplier.
  https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities/

MAINS AND ISOLATION

- Fine-pitch low-voltage rules do not apply indiscriminately to mains nets.
  The native rule file defines an 8 mm live-to-isolated corridor and larger
  clearances for full-voltage and divider nets. Preserve these rules on every
  copper layer; do not weaken them to remove routing violations.
- The spacing figures are prototype design constraints. Establish the final
  insulation requirements from working/impulse voltage, overvoltage category,
  material group, pollution environment, altitude and applicable product
  requirements. Solder mask does not automatically replace required spacing.
- Preserve the barrier through mounting hardware, wiring, solder joints,
  connector plugs, enclosure surfaces and manufacturing tolerances.

THERMAL AND ASSEMBLY

- R2 requires at least 300 mm2 combined pad/trace copper for its stated rating.
  Check actual filled copper after every routing or zone change.
- The conservative OLED/expansion power reservation is 5.7 W. Measure PSU
  local ambient at <=50 C and validate component temperatures in the final
  closed starter, including the continuous preload resistor.
- Independently review all derived footprints against manufacturer drawings:
  RACM06E supply, ESP32 module, ADuM6421A, fuse, fusible resistor, MOV and the
  modified G5Q relay pad geometry. Confirm purchased connector variants and
  mating harness drawings. Verify plug-entry direction and full mating-plug /
  wire-bend envelopes against nearby components; footprint courtyard clearance
  alone does not establish service access or enclosure fit.
- Confirm stencil/paste treatment and exposed-pad soldering for the buck and
  metering IC. Add assembly-panel tooling/fiducials with the chosen assembler;
  the small board outline is not an assembly-panel definition.

Use the exact final native DRC, unconnected-item and source-net comparison
reports. Zero CAD violations does not establish mains, EMC, thermal or pump
protection performance.
