PUMP CONTROLLER — OPERATING CONTRACT AND BRING-UP PLAN
Revision: 2026-11-19 / Rev E separate-panel engineering prototype

STATUS

This document specifies intended firmware behavior and commissioning checks.
Zigbee/Matter firmware, the local dashboard, control logic and calibration software
have not been implemented or tested. The circuit and PCB are a prototype;
schematic generation and CAD checks do not establish mains safety, EMC,
protection performance or compatibility with every starter.

Application assumed: one 220–240 VAC, single-phase, 1 HP pump, with an existing
DOL contactor and correctly selected motor overload protection. The board's
two series relay contacts control the contactor coil circuit. They do not
switch the motor power conductors. Confirm actual coil voltage, pickup VA,
holding VA and suppression before connecting it. Retain protective earth,
upstream disconnection, circuit protection and the motor overload device.

STARTER-SPECIFIC INTEGRATION HOLD

The supplied reference box is a capacitor-equipped single-phase submersible
starter with two large capacitors, a two-pole contactor, lid meters/MCB and an
external RMG controller. Recorded existing operation confirms that RMG AUTO starts the pump without
pressing the green START button. The automatic capacitor-start engagement/
disconnection circuit and its location have not been identified. A START button may operate that circuit as well as
the contactor. J30 only switches the contactor-coil command; it must not be
assumed to replace an automatic starting-capacitor sequence. Preserve all
motor/capacitor power wiring until the actual sequence is traced and verified.

A related RMG DFAWLC-044 manual uses separate starter relay pairs, and the
catalogue distinguishes start/stop from start/run/stop relay variants. The
installed variant is unconfirmed; this board is not yet a verified drop-in
replacement. Related sources, not proof of installed model identity:
https://rmgautomation.com/Downloads/Manual/DFAWLC-044-100101.pdf
https://rmgautomation.com/Wired_Automatic_Water_Level_Controller

Identify the existing RMG output/starting arrangement and actual coil voltage,
and verify motor overload protection; the visible MCB alone is not evidence
of the required overload function. No photo-derived wire colour or apparent
terminal position is an approved wiring mapping. These findings are an
integration hold, not a claim that the existing installation is defective.

LOCAL CONNECTION AND APP BEHAVIOR

Native Zigbee is the primary firmware target on ESP32-C6-MINI-1U-H8. Retain
Matter over Thread and Matter over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi as separate alternative builds;
select one operational transport per image. See wireless-architecture.rst and
wireless-variants.json for the detailed plan, sources and implementation status.
Local protection, physical controls and armed AUTO operation must work without
any network. Radio adapters submit requests to local control, never relay GPIO.

The identified Aqara M200 is a Matter controller and Thread border router.
Its published Zigbee support accepts Aqara child devices only, so direct pairing
of this custom Zigbee controller is unsupported. The native Zigbee build needs
a coordinator accepting the custom endpoint/profile. Matter/Thread is the
planned M200 integration route; Matter/Wi-Fi is retained. Exact endpoint and
hub interoperability remain untested. Apple Home integration likewise uses a
Matter build, not direct Zigbee. [1, 2, 3, 8]

Once commissioned, local network control must work with WAN disconnected while
the needed coordinator/controller, local network and border router stay powered.
Setup, account services, remote access, updates and some voice functions may
need internet. Test the selected variant's network and WAN failures separately.
All variants use the OLED for detailed water band, V/A/W/PF, validity and faults.
A local browser dashboard is planned only for the Matter/Wi-Fi build. Native
Apple Home or Aqara tiles for arbitrary litres, volts, amps and watts are not
guaranteed by the protocol's attributes. [1, 2]

Place the approved external antenna outside the metal starter enclosure using
an insulated mounting arrangement; keep its cable in the isolated low-voltage
compartment and observe the module manufacturer's antenna restrictions. [4]

Proposed app name: "Pump enable". ON means an enabled request, subject to the
physical mode, arm latch, water level and every protective inhibit. In AUTO,
an ON request can legitimately wait with the motor stopped because the tank
is full. The dashboard must distinguish enabled request, commanded relays,
measured running state, mode and reason for inhibition. A relay command alone
must never be presented as proof that the motor is running.

OFF from any accepted local/app command cancels the enabled request and keeps
it cancelled until a fresh ON/START. AUTO must not undo a user OFF on its next
scan. App ON never overrides physical STOP, an open high-high loop, a latched
fault, configuration mode or the need for physical re-arm.

PHYSICAL CONTROLS, MODE BUTTON AND RE-ARM

Rev E uses a separate panel with SW1 STOP (NC), SW2 START, SW3 MODE, SW4
SET/RESET and SW5 NEXT/+. Main-board SW3/SW4 are removed. Main SW1 RESET and
SW2 BOOT remain programming/recovery controls. There is no rotary OFF selector.
A factory-made GH12 cable connects main J32.n to panel J1.n, pins 1 through 12:

  1 +5V       2 STOP_RETURN    3 MODE/MAN_RETURN   4 NEXT/AUTO_RETURN
  5 START_N   6 SET/RESET_N    7 GND               8 +3V3
  9 I2C_SDA  10 I2C_SCL       11 KEY_MAN_N        12 KEY_AUTO_N

Host pins 11/12 retain the native names SPARE_B6/SPARE_B7. They are dedicated
to key sensing when that optional feature is enabled. The former J33/J34
headers are absent. Detailed signal assignments are in rev-e-integration.rst.

Local panel STOP and external panel J3 NC STOP are in series. Fit JP1 only
when the external STOP is absent; it never bypasses the onboard STOP. Other
external contacts parallel their corresponding local NO key:

  Panel J2 START: pin 1 START_N to pin 2 GND
  Panel J3 STOP:  pin 1 STOP_LOCAL_NC to pin 2 STOP_RETURN, NC contact
  Panel J4 MODE:  pin 1 MAN_RETURN to pin 2 STOP_RETURN
  Panel J5 SET:   pin 1 RESET_N to pin 2 GND
  Panel J6 NEXT:  pin 1 AUTO_RETURN to pin 2 STOP_RETURN

Retain the original START/STOP actuators and necessary power/capacitor-start
contacts. Where mechanically and electrically compatible, fit separate isolated
auxiliary contacts on those same actuators: NC STOP to panel J3, NO START to
panel J2. Do not connect the panel to any contact carrying mains or a
capacitor-start feed.
If the actual actuators cannot accept suitable separate contacts, the mechanical
control arrangement needs revision before installation; shared contacts are not
an acceptable substitute. The original power/capacitor function must be traced
and preserved without allowing any START/holding path to bypass J30.

STOP_RETURN directly supplies J31.1 and the independent high-high loop. D33
and D34 are removed. MODE and panel NEXT are sense-only inputs; neither
supplies relay-coil power. STOP opens the hardware permission supply and clears
ARM. Closing STOP or reclosing a high-high float must not itself restart the
pump. A held START through boot or fault recovery is not a fresh START.

Press AND RELEASE physical STOP before changing mode or entering settings.
This clears ARM. Releasing STOP restores the low-voltage supply used by MODE
and NEXT, while ARM remains clear. Do not require STOP to remain held during
mode selection or menu navigation: those inputs would be unpowered.

A fresh MODE press toggles MANUAL/AUTO only while stopped and unarmed. Display
the selected mode explicitly. MODE never arms or starts. A subsequent fresh
START arms a healthy controller and operates the selected mode. MANUAL obeys
STOP, high-high, destination-full stop, source availability, dry-run, metering/
sensor validity and electrical protections. AUTO fills below the commissioned
low band and stops at the high band. A normal full-tank stop may retain healthy
ARM for the next fill; a protective trip may not. AUTO-selected/unarmed must
look distinct from AUTO-enabled/waiting-for-low.

If MODE is pressed while armed, cancel the run/enable request, command outputs
off and show PRESS STOP. Do not toggle mode until a fresh physical STOP cycle
has cleared ARM and a fresh MODE press follows. A software response to MODE is
not claimed to clear the hardware ARM latch. Never derive ARM_CMD from MODE,
NEXT, SET, menu exit, or release of STOP.

Require all NO inputs to be released before accepting a new event after boot,
menu exit or a fault. Reject stuck inputs, ambiguous simultaneous button events
and stale control data; cancel requests and require release plus the normal
fresh STOP/START sequence. Debounce must not delay physical STOP/high-high.

OPTIONAL MAINTAINED KEY

Panel J8 accepts a break-before-make SPDT key:1 = +5V common,2 = MAN raw,3 = AUTO raw.
Enable it explicitly only after wiring/logic validation. Host GPB6/7 must be
inputs with internal pullups disabled. Active-low MAN/AUTO states 0/1 and1/0
select the mode; 0/0 and 1/1 inhibit starting. MODE must not override the enabled
maintained key. A key transition cancels run demand and disarms; require the
defined STOP acknowledgement and fresh START before operation. Neither a new
key position nor restored wiring may start or re-arm. Unplugged key normally
reads 1/1, unplugged whole panel 0/0; not every individual wire fault is detected.

PANEL SETTINGS KEYS AND OLED MENU

Panel SW4 SET/RESET short press acknowledges a displayed fault only after its cause
is gone and required healthy observations have completed. It never re-arms.
Hold SET to enter settings only after physical STOP press/release, with the
motor confirmed stopped, ARM clear and no unresolved fault. An armed/full-tank
standby or running state must instead show PRESS STOP and reject editing.

Inside settings:
  Panel SW5 NEXT short press = next item or increase the selected value.
  Panel SW5 NEXT held press  = previous item or decrease the selected value.
  Panel SW4 SET short press  = select/edit, or validate and save the edited value.
  Panel SW4 SET held press   = back/exit; do not save an unconfirmed edit.

One press must never generate both short and held actions. Consume the short
event once a hold is recognized; emit a short event only after release below
the hold threshold. Fault acknowledgement has priority over ordinary menu/page
actions. Front MODE does not navigate a menu; reject a mode change until the
menu is exited. START never navigates or runs from a menu. After exit require
all button releases and a fresh START; saving or exiting never resumes pumping.
STOP remains a physical stop in every screen state.

Show one setting/value with clear edit state, validation feedback and saved
confirmation. Use the same bounds as the local dashboard. No setting can
disable STOP, high-high, watchdog, fresh re-arm or mandatory protection checks.
Incomplete commissioning inhibits run. Loss of the display while editing
cancels the edit and leaves the controller unarmed; a stuck shared bus remains
a protective fault.

SET and NEXT are on the low-voltage panel PCB. Use verified insulated external
plungers where provided. If reaching a bare key requires opening the enclosure,
use the isolated service procedure with mains and coil supplies disconnected.
Exposed mains components are not a normal user-access area. The main J2 service
header is now 2 x 4 at 2.54 mm pitch; pin functions remain as documented in
programmability.txt, including ARM_TEST on pin 4 and no USB VBUS pin.

The SN74LVC1G74 ARM latch is clocked only by a deliberate ARM_CMD pulse after
a valid START. HEARTBEAT never clocks it. Supervisor, watchdog, external MCU_EN
reset and physical interlock clear permission through U36. Internal CPU/system
resets need not assert MCU_EN and may leave ARM_Q set. At every boot establish
a hardware-clear event before resuming a session; a verified implementation
can withhold HEARTBEAT longer than its 2.24 s maximum timeout before accepting
a fresh START. ARM_CMD low alone does not clear the latch.
Drop PERMIT_D and RUN_CMD on every
software fault, clear the request, and never pulse ARM_CMD merely because the
loop recovers. Heartbeat advances only after a complete healthy control cycle;
a free-running interrupt must not conceal stalled control or stale sensors.
Test firmware brownout plus the complete rail-collapse response.

A power cycle is not a fault reset or instruction to resume. Network restoration
must not re-arm a stopped controller. Healthy automatic operation continues
without Wi-Fi/WAN; firmware reboot leaves the pump off/unarmed. Dry-run retries
default to zero. Once locally armed, ordinary app OFF/ON can stop/request
operation without clearing a healthy ARM latch, but cannot clear faults, exit
configuration or satisfy physical re-arm requirements.

PROBES, WATER DISPLAY AND AUTOMATIC FILLING

The existing five tank wires are assumed to be COM plus four electrodes
L1, L2, L3, L4 in ascending height. COM must remain immersed below L1.
Confirm those identities by measurement before connection; five wires alone
do not prove this arrangement. J21 inlet sensing requires an inlet electrode
AND a valid nearby inlet-common electrode/return. A single existing inlet
wire is insufficient until its return is identified. Never use mains neutral,
protective earth, a motor body or an unverified pipe as the probe return.

For bits ordered L1,L2,L3,L4, the valid settled wet patterns are:
  0000  below L1
  1000  between L1 and L2
  1100  between L2 and L3
  1110  between L3 and L4
  1111  at or above L4
Other patterns, uncertain amplitudes, stale samples and failed excitation
self-checks require an explicit unknown/fault display, not a fabricated level.
Probe transitions need bounded debounce to reject splashes; the independent
high-high float and STOP must not wait for that debounce.

Four threshold probes give five bands, not continuous depth. Calibrate their
heights against tank geometry before showing volume intervals. Do not label
them exact 0/25/50/75/100% or exact litres without that mapping. Below L1 is not
necessarily empty, and reaching L4 does not establish the tank's total volume.
The OLED should show the current valid band, measured running state, mode and
fault status. Rotate readable pages for level/state and V/A/W/PF values;
faults take priority over page rotation. Show "unknown" for invalid data.
Page content, order, brightness and timing are firmware/configuration choices.
There are no separate level/status LEDs in the selected design.

The 128 x 32 OLED shares I2C with essential probe/control peripherals. A whole
512-byte screen transfer takes roughly 12–13 ms at 400 kHz and must not block
the 2 ms probe sampling slots. Queue at most 16 display-data bytes per short
transaction (about 0.405 ms wire time including address/control bytes), admit
it only when the sensor scheduler has adequate slack, and yield between chunks.
Refresh values at 1–2 Hz; split command sequences as well as pixel writes.
Rendering reads a state snapshot and has no authority to write motor outputs.
An OLED-only NACK may disable the display while valid control continues; a
stuck shared bus or stale essential data must cause a latched protective stop.
Bus operations require deadlines; if the control loop stalls, the hardware
watchdog must remove permission. See oled-selection.txt for harness and tests.

Simple conductivity probes cannot distinguish every broken wire from dry
water, or every short/fouled probe from a wet probe. Conductivity depends on
water and cable conditions. Use the independent normally closed high-high
float to interrupt permission where overflow consequences require it. A
shorting link in J31 is only a bypass for controlled bench work and provides
no independent high-high protection. A wet inlet probe indicates water at
its location; it does not prove pump flow or successful priming.

The proposed excitation is balanced 25 Hz AC. Follow probe-front-end.txt for
phase-locked sampling, mux settling, fresh conversions and bias/excitation
checks. Do not run protective decisions from cached ADC or meter values.

CONFIGURABLE INLET-SENSOR ROLE

Commission the inlet input's role; a probe in a normally flooded suction
source and a probe in a pipe that fills only after startup are not equivalent.

- SOURCE_PRESENT: require a healthy wet indication before starting, and stop
  on sustained loss of water. Use this only where the electrode location is
  expected to stay flooded while source water is available.
- PIPE_WATER_PROOF: an initially dry pipe does not by itself inhibit every
  start. Permit only a bounded, deliberately enabled start with all other
  protections healthy; require the probe to become wet within a commissioned
  proof deadline. The deadline must fit the pump maker's allowable priming
  conditions. Failure to become wet, or subsequent sustained loss of wet
  indication, latches a stop. No unlimited retries are permitted.

An unset role or proof deadline disables unattended AUTO. The provisional
one-second inlet-dry debounce applies only when the selected role expects
water; it does not override a validated startup-proof window. Neither role
makes a wet electrode proof of actual flow. Keep calibrated power-based
underload detection and all physical stop/overload protections active under
their own validated startup rules. Selecting a role changes firmware settings,
not the electrical probe circuit.

DRY-RUN AND ELECTRICAL COMMISSIONING

Use calibrated real input power as the principal underload signal, with
current and power factor available for diagnosis. A universal "1 HP dry-run
current" is invalid: load depends on the actual motor, voltage, pump curve,
head, valve setting and water condition. Low real power can also indicate
closed-valve operation, loss of prime or a broken coupling. The software must
report an underload/dry-run fault without claiming it uniquely identifies the
physical cause.

Calibrate RMS voltage, RMS current, phase and active power against suitable
reference instruments at representative loads. Record normal wet operating
power across the intended head and supply range, including the lowest normal
power case. Select an underload threshold below all validated normal cases
and above the validated fault cases with allowance for measurement error.
If those ranges overlap, power alone cannot reliably protect that installation;
add an independent suitable flow/pressure input in a revised design.

Do not deliberately run a pump dry merely to obtain a calibration number.
Use manufacturer-approved test conditions, a controlled test arrangement or
validated reference traces. Inrush blanking and priming grace apply only to
the underload algorithm. STOP, high-high, invalid data and independent motor
overload protection remain effective during startup. Limit any priming grace
to the pump manufacturer's permitted condition and time.

PROVISIONAL COMMISSIONING SETTINGS — NOT VALIDATED PROTECTION VALUES

These values are engineering starting points for bench tests. Store final
settings with a version, range checking and calibration record. AUTO and
unattended MAN operation remain disabled until the installation is validated.

  Control cycle:              50 ms target; bounded deadline.
  Watchdog heartbeat:         every 250 ms after a completed healthy cycle.
  Probe sample averaging:     5 excitation cycles, approximately 200 ms.
  Excitation startup discard: at least 1 s, followed by valid self-checks.
  Probe wet/dry amplitudes:   >=100 mV / <=60 mV provisional; middle uncertain.
  Level transition debounce: 2 s maximum before stable classification.
  Inlet-dry debounce:         1 s provisional when the configured role expects
                             water; see the bounded startup-proof role above.
  Stale probe/meter data:     >1 s since a required valid result: latched stop.
  AUTO start:                L1 dry for 2 s, with all other conditions healthy.
  Normal full stop:           L4 wet for 2 s; independent HH acts immediately.
  Minimum off interval:      30 s provisional; increase to maker's limit.
  Minimum run interval:      none may postpone a stop or protective trip.
  Underload startup blank:   1 s provisional for inrush, within total grace.
  Total priming grace:       5 s provisional from the start command; shorten
                             if required by the pump manufacturer.
  Sustained underload time:  3 s after grace, subject to allowed damage time.
  Initial threshold study:   0.65 x measured normal wet power is a trial
                             candidate only; do not enable without validation.
  Automatic fault retries:  0; SET/RESET acknowledgement then fresh START
                             after fault clearance.

Set a maximum continuous fill time from measured worst-case legitimate fill
time and pump duty limits. A provisional study value is 1.5 times the longest
validated fill time, capped by permitted motor duty; an unset limit must
prevent unattended AUTO rather than mean unlimited run. Set voltage and
overcurrent thresholds from motor nameplate/manufacturer limits and measured
startup behavior, not generic values inferred from 1 HP. Existing thermal
overload protection remains required.

Detect failure to establish running power and power persisting after a stop.
Determine their time limits from measured contactor and motor behavior. If
current persists after both relays are commanded off, latch a fault and show
"motor still powered"; the board cannot guarantee interruption of a welded
external contactor. Use the upstream disconnect before investigation.

ORDERED BRING-UP AND ACCEPTANCE CHECKS

1. Unpowered inspection. Check every actual component against its datasheet,
   including supervisor pinout, relay coil voltage, connectors, isolation
   barrier, slots, spacing, mounting hardware and field-wire separation.
   Check the assembled board for shorts and wrong resistor values. Verify
   upstream fuse coordination for the installation's prospective fault
   current; the on-board fuse rating alone does not establish board SCCR.

2. Isolated low-voltage supply only. Keep all mains terminals and contactor
   wiring disconnected. Feed +5V/GND through a current-limited bench supply
   with the AC/DC source unpowered and an approved isolation arrangement.
   Start at a low current limit and inspect consumption, 3.3 V and heating.
   The permanent 33 ohm PSU bleeder draws about 152 mA at 5 V by itself.
   Check buck startup and ripple before inserting expensive modules if
   assembly permits. Never back-feed a powered AC/DC module from USB/debug.

3. Supply and reset tests. With dummy loads, sweep the allowed 5 V range,
   apply MCU/Wi-Fi load steps, and scope +3V3, SAFE_CLEAR_N, ARM_CLEAR_N, ARM_Q, PERMIT_Q
   and both coil drives. Test slow ramps, fast dips, power interruption and
   recovery. Verify zero unintended pickup and no automatic re-arm. Test
   the new supervisor threshold and release delay, finite reset propagation,
   watchdog timing, all internal/external reset modes, U36 conditioning and
   actual relay pickup/release at temperature. Follow rev-e-electrical-review.rst. The
   nominal voltage/tolerance calculation does not replace those tests.

4. Hardware stop chain without a motor. Use the actual three-button front panel, internal keys and
   high-high float. Test STOP and high-high independently with dummy relay
   loads; restore each and confirm a fresh START is needed. Verify that mode
   inputs are sense-only and cannot energize or clear ARM. Test ambiguous
   simultaneous events, MODE while armed, STOP press/release before navigation,
   short/held SET and NEXT event exclusion, invalid saves and no resume on menu exit.
   Hold START through boot and fault recovery. Force the MCU to reset, stall
   the control task and stop sensor updates while a timer still runs. None
   may leave K1 permitted beyond the documented protective deadline. Verify
   no external mains control wire is connected to J31/J32 or probe headers.

5. Probe simulator, then real water. Exercise every valid/invalid pattern,
   open/shorted wires, missing common, phase loss, ADC errors and long cable
   loading. Check amplitudes and electrode AC balance with suitable isolated
   instruments. Repeat with the installation's water and full cable length.
   Confirm inlet return and whether an independent HH float is installed.

6. Meter and application tests at low risk. Use a controlled isolated test
   setup to calibrate the metering chain and confirm CT orientation and
   burden. Never open the CT secondary while its primary carries current.
   Simulate calibrated underload/overcurrent/voltage faults before involving
   a pump. Verify every timeout, output state, SET/RESET versus START distinction,
   short/hold exclusion,
   history timestamp and unknown-data indication.

7. Network acceptance with relay dummy loads for each implemented variant.
   For native Zigbee, use an explicitly compatible coordinator; M200 direct
   custom-Zigbee pairing is unsupported. For Matter, pair with the actual
   Apple Home/HomePod and M200 combinations. Confirm ON/OFF semantics in MAN
   and AUTO. Disconnect WAN, the active radio network, and then hubs in turn.
   Physical controls/protection must continue; lost connectivity never causes
   a restart or fabricated telemetry. Test late/repeated commands, mode
   changes, fresh physical re-arm, reboot, update, rollback and pairing reset.
   Updates, factory reset and transport-image changes first stop and unarm.

8. Qualified mains bench validation. Only after the earlier checks, use a
   qualified electrical test setup to validate insulation, protective earth,
   leakage, actual creepage/clearance, dielectric withstand and the required
   EMC/surge/EFT behavior under the applicable equipment requirements. Verify
   coil suppression and contact switching with the real DOL coil. Do not
   attach an earth-referenced oscilloscope or ordinary USB lead to the live
   meter domain. Follow an approved isolated measurement method.

9. Thermal/full-load check in the closed starter enclosure. Operate both
   relays, Wi-Fi transmit bursts, OLED worst-case pattern and metering. The
   conservative 5 V budget is 5.81 W including the minimum-load resistor,
   100 mA OLED reservation, 25 mA at 3.3 V spare/key allowance, panel/key loads
   and the revised reset-stage reserve. Check both 1.2 A and 6 W limits; keep
   the AC/DC module local ambient at or below 50 degrees C. At 65 degrees C
   its 5 W derated output is insufficient for that budget. Verify the 33 ohm
   resistor's 0.677–0.844 W dissipation, required heat-spreading copper and
   temperatures of nearby probes, RF module, relay coils and capacitors.
   Keep the bleeder's heat away from measurement and RF parts. A closed box
   can be materially hotter than the surrounding room.

10. Supervised pump commissioning. Fit the correctly rated overload, verify
    rotation/flow/prime by approved means, measure wet running ranges, record
    fill times and validate limits. Confirm high and low electrode heights,
    HH float action, stop release and restart inhibition. Record the final
    settings and test results before enabling unattended operation. Fault
    injection must remain within the pump and test-equipment limits.

CONTROL-INPUT CONDITIONING ACCEPTANCE
U37 SN74LVC3G17DCUR now separates the 5 V-derived STOP/MODE/NEXT raw dividers
from U33. R80/R82/R84 are 10 kohm; their 33 kohm pull-downs and 10 nF filters remain.
Measure each RAW node and corresponding U33 input during button operation,
open harnesses, supply extremes and contactor interference. Polarity is
unchanged. Require stable debounced states and disable U33 internal pull-ups
on these externally driven inputs. The 3.3 V rail and all three outputs must be
checked through collapse/recovery; Ioff at 0 V does not define the entire ramp.
See rev-e-electrical-review.rst E11 and bench acceptance T01/T05.
