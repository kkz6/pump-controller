VERIFICATION STATUS - REV C - 19 November 2026

Engineering prototype, not a manufacturing or mains-installation release.
Final native counts and source hashes are recorded in hardware/checks.
No assembled controller, firmware or starter integration has been tested.

Revision scope
- Retained START/STOP actuators with suitable isolated auxiliary contacts,
  one new MODE button, and a small OLED behind a lid cut-out.
- D33/D34 removed; STOP_RETURN directly feeds J31 high-high loop.
- SW3 SET/RESET and SW4 NEXT/+ added on the PCB using existing MCP inputs.
  MODE, menus and fault acknowledgement cannot arm or start the motor.
- The photographed starter contains capacitors and a two-pole contactor.
  Its current system starts automatically. The start-capacitor switching
  provider, coil supply, overload path and existing wire identities are not
  traced; J30 is not a demonstrated drop-in replacement for RMG relay pairs.

CAD verification
- Source-defined connected pins are compared with the native schematic netlist.
- Native schematic ERC and native PCB DRC reports are supplied, including
  separate final DRC and explicit unconnected-item checks.
- Board pad nets and footprints are compared with the circuit source.
- Live-to-isolated rules, filled R2 copper and via-in-pad inventory are checked.
- No project exclusions may hide shorts, clearance or unconnected items.
- Three-dimensional component positions follow the PCB. Ten component bodies
  are approximate envelopes; the enclosure scene is a fit proposal only.

Required physical/firmware verification
- Independent land-pattern, mains insulation, protective-device coordination,
  actual coil switching/inrush and motor-start sequence review.
- Meter calibration, current-path identification before capacitor/winding
  branches, startup clipping, loaded/dry pump and probe/cable characterization.
- Boot, brownout, watchdog, physical STOP/high-high and input fault injection.
  Include very short STOP presses, held START/MODE, failed I2C and menu exit.
- Internal tactile contact reliability, debounce and mutually exclusive short/
  held actions. Internal keys require isolated service power/access; their
  low-voltage location does not make an exposed live starter safe to touch.
- Temperature in the actual closed starter, including the preload resistor,
  hot relay pickup and full STOP/high-high cable loop. PSU local ambient<=50C.
- Actual OLED loom length/hinge slack, shared-bus timing, noise and fault recovery.
- Firmware implementation, local Matter interoperability, RF, surge and EMC.

The board does not directly sense overload trip or contactor auxiliary state.
Not every external coil-loop interruption clears the hardware ARM latch.
Tank probes give discrete level bands; inlet conductivity is not flow proof.
Historical archive checks apply only to their explicitly recorded revision.
