VERIFICATION STATUS — 19 November 2026

This package is the active Rev B engineering prototype draft. It is not a
manufacturing release. Review/calculation is not an assembled-hardware test,
production approval or installation acceptance.

Completed within the electrical review:
- Manufacturer pin/connection cross-checks of the probe front end, metering
  interface, local I/O and independent ARM/watchdog/interlock logic.
- Ideal meter input-range and resistor-power calculations.
- Linear RC probe simulation for the stated water resistance, cable
  capacitance, sampling schedule and selected component/supply corners.
- Limited static input and relay supply-margin calculations; limitations
  are recorded in core-review.txt and probe-calculation.txt.

Resolved value/pin changes checked in merged circuit.json:
- U32 is STM1001TWX6F, RESET1/VCC2/GND3. Its 3.15V maximum threshold
  leaves 34.5mV below the regulator static minimum. Dynamic brownout/ripple
  behavior remains unqualified; this is not a complete corner guarantee.
- R87 is 100k, increasing the HH Schmitt input margin. Off-state discharge
  can have a 1ms time constant; selector OFF dwell must be verified.
- MCP spare GPB6/7 use separate 100k pulldowns, not hard grounding.
- The indicator panel is replaced by a 0.91-inch 128x32 I2C OLED at J33.
  GPA0..GPA7 are intentional unconnected inputs with firmware pullups.
  J33=3V3/GND/SDA/SCL; selected carrier=SCL/SDA/GND/VCC. The harness
  must map signals explicitly. Prior panel-board files are superseded.
- Display bus-time/pullup calculations are documented, but the updated
  screen has not been tested with the probe ADC or protection firmware.
  The agreed schedule uses at most 16 data bytes per display transaction,
  a measured <=0.5ms transaction target, and 1-2Hz normal refresh. Up to
  5Hz is conditional on the validated shared-bus/control timing budget.

Required before powered installation:
- Independent schematic/PCB isolation, creepage/clearance, fuse, connector,
  protective-earth and actual contactor-coil compatibility review.
- Current/voltage calibration, startup/inrush clipping and loaded/dry pump
  characterization; no universal dry-run threshold has been established.
- Actual water/probe/cable tests, open/short fault behavior and sensor aging.
- Boot/brownout/watchdog/STOP/OFF/high-high fault-injection tests; no automatic
  re-arm from ordinary heartbeat or restored input/supply is permitted.
- Combined supply/transient/temperature tests in the closed starter, including
  relay hot pickup and the full high-high cable loop. The present K1 OR-diode
  path has limited hot-pickup margin; 50C/60C operation is not qualified.
- Firmware implementation and validation of input debounce, invalid-mode
  fail-off, fresh-user ARM, local Manual/Auto behavior and local Matter control.
- OLED traffic scheduling, shared-bus fault recovery, stale ADC/input fail-off,
  rise time/LOW level and analog-noise tests with the actual display/harness.
- EMC, surge/ESD and applicable equipment-level electrical safety testing.

The current board does not directly monitor the overload trip or contactor
auxiliary state. External coil-loop interruptions are not all hardware-latched.
Four tank electrodes provide discrete level bands, not continuous litres.
A conductive inlet detector indicates water presence, not verified flow.
