PUMP CONTROLLER — OLED SELECTION, HARNESS AND TIMING
Revision: 2026-11-19 / Rev E engineering prototype

SELECTED MODULE

Waveshare SKU 14657, part name "0.91inch OLED Module": white monochrome
128 x 32 pixels, SSD1306 controller, I2C interface. Manufacturer dimensions
are 36.0 x 12.5 mm for the module and 22.38 x 5.58 mm for the active display.
The selected module replaces the discrete level/status indicator panel.
It is a purchased module mounted behind a protected front-panel window;
the controller PCB needs only its four-wire harness. Confirm actual thickness,
connector/cable clearance and mechanical retention on the received module
before finalizing the enclosure cutout. The 36 x 12.5 figure is not a complete
three-dimensional installation envelope. [1]

Manufacturer product/wiki information supports nominal 3.3 V or 5 V input.
Use the controller's isolated +3V3 rail here. The published module schematic
contains an XC6206P332MR regulator, SSD1306 charge-pump capacitors, reset RC
and 4.7 kohm pull-ups on each I2C line to the module's internal 3V3 node.
At 3.3 V input that internal node will be slightly lower because of regulator
dropout. Verify operation at controller rail corners and startup; the regulator
does not create an additional isolation barrier. [1, 2]

No guaranteed maximum whole-module current is published in the official
product/wiki/manual/schematic information checked. Reserve 100 mA at 3.3 V
as an engineering allowance pending measurements. This is not a datasheet
maximum. Measure startup peaks, full-white pixels, highest allowed contrast,
temperature and supply corners. Chip logic-current figures do not include
the complete OLED panel and charge-pump load.

With 100 mA reserved for the OLED and 25 mA for spare/key 3.3 V loads, the 3.3 V
base reservation is 655 mA, plus 9.01 mA reserved for the revised reset biases,
clear-stage and three-channel input-buffer transition current (round total to 665 mA). Rev E has no physical
J34 expansion connector. Including the panel/key and reset-stage loads, the conservative 5 V allocation is 5.81 W, leaving
0.19 W against the 6 W supply at no more than 50 degrees C local module ambient. This is
a narrow reserve; actual OLED current and charge-pump load steps need bench
verification. See power-budget-review.txt for the original allocation and
rev-e-electrical-review.rst for the current reset/input-buffer delta and limits.

CONNECTORS AND EXACT WIRE MAPPING

Rev E uses a direct one-to-one GH 1.25 mm twelve-way cable from main J32 to panel
J1. The horizontal receptacle at each end is SM12B-GHS-TB(LF)(SN), mating with
GHR-12V-S housings. Nominal host cable length is 150 mm. Main J33/J34 were removed;
the old reversed four-wire J33 loom is not compatible with this revision.

Panel J7 is the horizontal PH 2.0 mm four-way S4B-PH-SM4-TB(LF)(SN). Its numeric
order matches the Waveshare module PH 2.0 mm J1 exactly, permitting a factory-made
100 mm PH4-to-PH4 cable with no installer soldering:

  Main J32 / panel J1      Panel J7       OLED module J1
        10 I2C_SCL            1 SCL             1 SCL
         9 I2C_SDA            2 SDA             2 SDA
         7 GND                3 GND             3 GND
         8 +3V3               4 VCC             4 VCC

Pololu 5623 is a procurement candidate for the PH-style 100 mm lead; verify the
actual connector fit and numeric 1-to-1 continuity at acceptance. The factory
harness alternative and exact cavity/contact specifications are recorded in
hardware/panel-rev-e/parts-and-interface.rst. A PH-to-loose-Dupont cable does
not meet this retained plug-to-plug interface. [1,2,4,5,6]

Use connector cavity numbers from mating-face drawings and continuity, not
wire colors or assumed left/right orientation. Both module connectors J1 and
P1 share the published numeric mapping. No hot plugging, separate 5 V supply,
or cable alongside motor/contactor leads. Keep all display wiring in the
isolated compartment with strain relief. Nominal 150 mm host+100 mm OLED gives a
250 mm total target; this is not a validated I2C cable-length limit.

I2C SIGNAL INTEGRITY AND ADDRESS PLAN

Use 7-bit addresses: MCP23017 0x20, ADS1115 0x48, OLED expected 0x3C; Waveshare
documents 0x3C for its 128 x 32 SSD1306 setup. Confirm the supplied unit before
commissioning. The shifted write byte 0x78 is not a different 7-bit address.
A future internal adapter/peripheral must use a nonconflicting address. [3]

The controller already has 3.3 kohm pull-ups. The module's 4.7 kohm pull-ups
give approximately 1.94 kohm combined pull-up resistance when their supply
nodes are close. Verify rise time, low-level sink current and noise with the
populated main/panel/OLED harness at 400 kHz. Fit no extra panel pullups.
Verify Fast-mode rise time<=300 ns; 3300 ohm alone gives an approximately 107 pF
nominal total-capacitance estimate using tr=0.8473*Rp*C. Module pullups change
the effective resistance and sink current, so measured rise time/low level
is the acceptance criterion. Do not lower the entire bus speed without
requalifying the 2 ms probe slots and bounded OLED chunks below.
Test unpowered-module behavior: the pull-ups and input
structures can back-feed an unpowered device. The normal installation powers
the module and controller together; avoid partial-power connection sequences.
Measure whether OLED charge-pump noise alters probe/meter readings.

DISPLAY SOFTWARE AND SHARED-BUS DEADLINES

Firmware remains unimplemented. Planned operation is a 512-byte monochrome
framebuffer with 1–2 Hz value refresh. Provide programmable rotating pages:
water band/estimated volume interval, operating mode and measured running
state, then voltage/current/real power/power factor. Display source-water and
sensor validity. Fault text overrides normal page rotation; clear any stale
numeric value or mark it unknown. A full screen need not show every value at
once. Larger text and page rotation keep normal readings clear. The front MODE button
toggles MANUAL/AUTO. Panel SW4 SET/RESET and SW5 NEXT/+ provide settings navigation.
Brightness, page order, units and intervals are configuration choices, subject
to display power limits. Dim during inactivity and shift/rotate content to
reduce persistent-image wear; important fault status must remain readable.

At 400 kHz, 512 data bytes plus one address and control byte occupy at least
514 x 9 / 400000 = 11.565 ms on the bus. Chunking into 16-byte writes makes
32 x 18 x 9 / 400000 = 12.96 ms, before extra addressing commands/software
overhead. Either is much longer than a 2 ms probe sampling slot. A conventional
blocking full-frame refresh is therefore unsuitable on this shared bus.

Use at most 16 data bytes per transaction: address + control + 16 data bytes
take approximately 0.405 ms wire time. Count actual START/STOP, driver and
command overhead too. A single bus scheduler owns access and admits an OLED
chunk only if enough slack remains before the next essential sensor deadline.
Yield after every transaction; do not queue the entire frame as one locked
operation. Split command sequences as well, retain the display address state,
and postpone display work if sensor sampling needs the bus. Bound every I2C
operation and check the actual timing with a logic analyzer. Target transaction
wall time <=0.5 ms only after measurement proves that bound; otherwise reduce
the chunk size or reserve more slack. Display 1–2 Hz is a refresh target, not
a justification to miss ADC deadlines.

The renderer consumes immutable/timestamped status and has no direct motor
output access. A missing OLED that simply NACKs can be reported locally over
the active transport or USB service and disabled without changing healthy
pump control. An OLED holding
SDA/SCL low affects essential MCP/ADC traffic: time out, invalidate stale
measurements and perform the specified protective stop and latched fault.
No busy retry loop may continue a false heartbeat. If a software deadlock
prevents the stop, the independent watchdog clears hardware permission.
Recovery of the bus alone must not re-arm the pump. Test display unplugged,
NACK, SDA stuck low, SCL stuck low, slow response and corrupted commands with
relay dummy loads before use with the contactor.

ENCLOSURE INTEGRATION
The 250 mm total cable target includes main-to-panel and panel-to-OLED wiring.
Check the actual case routing, retention and service slack, then qualify rise
time, noise and transaction deadlines with that completed harness. A 3D model
or assembled-looking image does not establish electrical cable qualification.
