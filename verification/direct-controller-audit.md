# Direct controller electrical audit

Reviewed design [`2e63957`](https://github.com/kkz6/pump-controller/commit/2e639575bab3c2bea0863739c2ff302c923780c9). Hold this revision for installed service. The required behavior is for the factory STOP and overload to interrupt the coil and for STOP release or overload reset to leave the motor off until a fresh start. The conventional interface passes the first condition in the contact model and fails the second.

## Findings

| Priority | Finding | Required evidence or change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blocker | In the conventional three-wire profile, the factory STOP and overload open the coil circuit. If K2 welds while K1 remains closed, releasing STOP or resetting overload starts the motor again. The motor also restarts with healthy relays when STOP is released during an active K2 pickup pulse. The model supplied fresh and accurate stopped feedback in both cases. | Factory STOP and overload must independently clear run authorization. Demonstrate that normal release, overload reset and power return cannot reapply an old pickup command. Repeat with a held or welded pickup contact. |
| Blocker for electronic START/STOP | A self-latching starter can continue running through a failed panel bus. In seven seconds of continuous invalid samples, the controller dropped the heartbeat and issued zero STOP pulses. The OLED, panel expander and probe ADC share that bus. Loss of controller power also removes its ability to issue STOP. | Verify the OEM interface and prove an independent interruption path for each supported electronic starter. Check fault handling with the bus stuck, the controller unplugged and USB connected. |
| High | During electronic control initialization, K2 emits a STOP pulse before arm. J30 and J35 use the same PCB header. Interchanging uncoded plugs made that pulse a START in the model. The field parts list includes two coding elements, so this case applies if coding is missing or ineffective. | Publish and inspect a physically distinct coding pattern for each mating pair. Confirm at assembly that the plugs cannot be interchanged. |
| High | A broken tank common can read as a valid empty tank and permit AUTO start. A broken top probe can read as the valid three-probe state while the tank is full. A maximum runtime cannot establish where the water is, and a normally loaded pump can still overflow the tank. The single inlet electrode also depends on electrical continuity through water to the remote common. | Define how AUTO handles an unplugged or broken probe loom. Check a full-tank top-electrode open, an empty tank, real flowing water and the timeout against actual fill rates. No separate high-high float is assumed. |
| High | The checked-in image is a USB bench build. The current driver reads voltage RMS only. The pump control component receives tank levels, motor-stopped status and process health from its caller. In host traces, an accepted START with no motor motion stayed RUNNING for ten seconds, and unexpected motion in IDLE produced no feedback fault. | Integrate tank acquisition, motor feedback, current or power based dry-run protection, OLED and offline communications in the operational firmware. Calibrate voltage and current, establish startup blanking and test start and stop confirmation. |
| High | The AC1020 is a 20 A CT. The specified 6.8 Ω harness burden and single-ended ADE7953 channel A give a nominal sine-wave limit of about 26 A RMS at gain 1. The CT may go out of range sooner. J10 is referenced to mains neutral, and the burden is a separate harness part. F1 has 200 A AC interrupting capacity. | Verify the captive burden and insulation of the complete CT harness. Measure running, dry-run and inrush behavior. Verify available fault current and coordination with the actual upstream protective device. |
| Integration | The optional key selects firmware mode. The BOM lists a separate passive restoration insert for a suitably wired three-wire starter when the factory controls need to work with the controller unplugged. The USB inhibit removes the relay coil supply. A welded contact or an already latched external starter can still conduct or run. | Confirm the actual coil voltage, inrush, capacitor start sequence, OEM command levels, restoration loom and starter behavior. Use a physically isolated bench setup for programming. |
| Integration | USB D+/D− routing diverges between the ESD part and data switch. One segment was about 10.8 mm versus 2.6 mm, and a subsequent segment changes layers on only one conductor. The 5 V preload dissipates about 0.76 W continuously. | Review the USB pair against the fabrication stack-up and verify enumeration in both orientations. Measure enclosure temperature and perform insulation and contactor-switching immunity tests with assembled hardware and the fitted loom. |
| Integration | Aqara documents bridging for its Zigbee devices and support for third-party Matter devices. The custom Zigbee project's M200 pairing and Apple Home bridging have no demonstrated result. The installed cost is also open. The recorded one-pair subtotal is ₹28,417.26 with several installation items still unquoted. | Demonstrate local control with the intended hub and without Internet. Confirm voltage and level visibility in Apple Home separately from basic control. Obtain a complete fitted quote before choosing quantities. |

## Reproduced checks

- KiCad 10.0.8 returned zero reported ERC and DRC violations, unconnected items or schematic parity discrepancies for both boards under the project rules. A copper geometry cross-check found about 8.06 mm minimum planar separation between the classified live and low-voltage copper in the main PCB. Installed insulation remains open, including components, harnesses, mounting hardware and enclosure.
- Both published Gerber archives passed nominal copper and plated-drill connectivity against IPC-D-356 with zero opens or shorts. The comparison detected a deliberate bridge and a deliberate open on each board. Published file hashes matched the recorded release.
- The existing firmware host suite passed. The direct contact model independently exposed five unsafe outcomes. Two comparison cases, healthy K2 after STOP release once pickup had finished and correctly keyed electronic wiring during initialization, ended OFF. The test provides a contactor model and uses the current controller C code. It does not represent a test of a physical contactor, coil, pump or exact OEM electronic starter.

Run the contact model from the project root with `sh verification/run-controller-contact-review.sh`. Exit status 1 reports violations on the reviewed design. Exit status 2 reports a setup failure.
