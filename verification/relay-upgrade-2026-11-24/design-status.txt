COMPONENT RATINGS AND APPLICATION LIMITS
The relay NO contact is rated 16 A and NC 5 A at 250 V AC resistive. The manufacturer's high-inrush tests specify peak current, RMS current and endurance conditions. The terminal's 24 A rating is also a component rating. No 16 A continuous board rating or blanket capacitor/motor rating is claimed from trace width or relay markings.

User application: 1.5 HP, 220 V capacitor-start starter. An external suitably rated starter interface/contactor is now allowed if the actual START contacts carry capacitor current; external interface design is deferred. Direct capacitor switching is not approved by this PCB review. The redesign is complete as an engineering prototype; qualify the selected interface's load, bounded start pulse, first-article temperature rise, STOP/overload and power-loss behavior before pump service. A larger relay and NC terminal do not create an independent cutoff for a welded capacitor contact.

Coil budget: 160 mA nominal for both relays versus the former 80 mA. With 5.25 V, -10% coil resistance and a copper-temperature estimate at -40 C, both coils together are about 248 mA. This is a screening estimate, not a manufacturer operating guarantee. Verify the complete 5 V rail peak/thermal budget on the 1.2 A RECOM supply. PMOS drain flyback and STOP-release waveforms need bench measurements.
