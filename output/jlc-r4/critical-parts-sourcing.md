**Purchasing decisions**

- For RV1, Littelfuse specifies the same crimped, inline 7.5 mm leads for L2B7 and L2T7. L2T7 is the tape-and-reel ordering option. DigiKey listed 4,984 of TMOV14RP320EL2T7. The library listed only three under Littelfuse/IXYS C22815876. Record this packaging approval on the assembly BOM before requesting it. [Littelfuse ordering codes](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/tmov-itmov?assetguid=bd475732-1071-4352-b8aa-f78b0007eb05) · [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/littelfuse-inc/TMOV14RP320EL2T7/2024645) · [JLCPCB](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/LittelfuseIXYS-TMOV14RP320EL2T7/C22815876)
- For U301, the hardware BOM specifies Texas Instruments LM393BDR. TI's ordering table identifies **LM393BIDR** for the active LM393B SOIC-8 and contains no entry for the former spelling. Verify the intended temperature grade, package and pinout before updating this protection-circuit BOM line. JLCPCB showed 100,841 of the documented part as C2865059. [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm393b.pdf) · [TI part record](https://www.ti.com/product/LM393B/part-details/LM393BIDR) · [JLCPCB](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/TexasInstruments-LM393BIDR/C2865059)
- JLC Global Sourcing and customer consignment require supplier acceptance, inventory receipt and matching against the assembly order. Distributor listings above establish sourcing leads only. Allow for assembly attrition, purchase minimums, transit and inspection when quoting. [JLCPCB sourcing](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcba-parts-sourcing-instruction) · [JLCPCB component matching](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/component-matching-guidelines-for-pcba-orders)

**Exact-part quotation requested**

On 20 November 2026 JLCPCB confirmed receipt of a nonbinding PCB Assembly Inquiry for two Main R4 and two Panel F boards. The subject was “RFQ: 22 exact parts for two complete pump-controller sets”. The attachment lists 22 original manufacturer part numbers covering 102 net fitted pieces across both sets. Those lines cover the unresolved component groups identified in the earlier five-pair assembly comparison. All quantities in the attachment were recalculated for two complete sets. [Exact parts and net quantities sent](exact-parts-rfq.csv)

The inquiry asks for exact-part stock, unit and extended prices, compulsory purchase minimums, assembly attrition, excess quantities, fees, duties and lead times to JLCPCB's assembly warehouse. It requests itemized landed costs to India or identification of delivery costs that require a postal code. Optional component spares were excluded. JLCPCB was asked to confirm every specified surface-mount and through-hole placement, including relays and connectors, and to identify any factory assembly exceptions. The marked 150 mm JST GH 12-position harness and Waveshare 14657 OLED with compatible lead were requested as separately priced options. Procurement, charging and production require separate purchase approval. The inquiry has no order number or supplier price commitment. [JLCPCB contact and inquiries](https://jlcpcb.com/help/contact)

**Reference prices and minimums**

A separate BOM Tool preview with the assembly quantity set to two matched 17 of the 22 requested MPNs for preorder. It suggested a different part for one capacitor and left four MPNs unmatched. Two RECOM modules showed USD 45.8846, and two ADUM isolators showed USD 34.6452, for a combined indicative USD 80.5298 before assembly and delivery. JLCPCB describes these preorder prices as indicative until after payment. The separate inquiry above requested written pricing without payment.
