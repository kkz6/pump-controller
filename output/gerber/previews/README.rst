Rev C Gerber previews
====================

These are views of the exported Rev C main-board Gerbers, not native PCB
screenshots. They do not include the separate front-panel PCB revision.
The source board SHA-256 is
``6f991556083c578a128364c8498850fce7301d18344051a5d814eb829f6d0b85``.

``top-registration`` and ``bottom-registration`` combine copper, solder mask,
silkscreen, paste and drill data. Both retain the same unmirrored manufacturing
coordinates to make registration easy to compare. The bottom image is not a
view looking at a physically flipped board.

``copper-L1`` through ``copper-L4`` show the four copper images in top-to-bottom
stack order. Brown is copper and white is its absence. Drill holes are not
subtracted in these individual copper images.

The SVGs are generated by ``tools/validate_gerbers.py`` with Gerbonara 0.13.0
and Python 3.10 or newer. The PNGs were rendered from those SVGs with Chrome.
All six PNGs were visually inspected for clipping, orientation and layer
registration. The independent parser report is
``hardware/fabrication/rev-c/checks/gerber-validation.json``.

These previews are for review. They are not masks to print, do not extract a
conductive Gerber netlist, and do not establish Robu manufacturing acceptance.
